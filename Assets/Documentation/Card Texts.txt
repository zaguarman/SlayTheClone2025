Its subfactions are [Offline], the mood of the times offline (films and memes everyone quotes, human archetypes, modern malaise), and [Online], the internet's (social media, influencers, the mob). Momentum, comebacks and swarms; it lives in the moment and pays with self-harm and exposure.
Myth: Faith, death and prophecy: legends, religion, monsters, the afterlife. Slow and inevitable: its creatures die and come back, its spells pay off Days later ([Prophecy]), its statuses are [Doomed] and [Blessed]. It pays with [Sacrifice] and it can't move its creatures. A status-quo faction: it wins by holding the board as it is.
Statecraft: Order, law and war: government, bureaucracy, the military, propaganda. It fortifies the line, slows everyone down with paperwork (Red Tape: [Deployment Time] on the enemy), then strikes first. It pays with bureaucracy (its own creatures deploy slowly) and it can't hide anything: all its plans are public.
Shadow Ops.: Secrets and sabotage: espionage, covert operations, conspiracies. Fragile agents, hidden plans ([Concealed], Undercover), traps and [Watchdog]s set up in advance. It wins the information war and loses fair fights. It pays by burning its own cards and it can't heal or gain [Armor]. A timing faction: it strikes hard in short windows.
Corporate: Money, growth and exploitation: business, finance, labour, organised crime. An engine: invest now, cash out later, spend cheap workers as fuel; it draws more cards than anyone. It pays with capital (discarding from its hand) and it can't heal: the insurance never pays out. A timing faction.
Tech: Systems and the queue: software, hacking, infrastructure. It rewires the Day with [Watchdog]s, copies and overrides, Speed boosts and automated units. It pays with technical debt (fewer draws next Day) and it can't bring anything back from the discard pile: deleted is deleted.
Offline: The culture itself, offline: films and memes everyone quotes, human archetypes, social trends, modern malaise. A subfaction of [Digital Society]. Momentum and comebacks: moods that buff a creature but burn it out, Underdog bonuses when losing, Trends that set the rules for everyone (Rush Hour), strikes that shut engines down. It pays with self-harm and it can't use Intelligence: it lives in the moment.
Online: The internet's culture: social media, influencers, cancel culture, memes, the mob. A subfaction of [Digital Society]. A swarm of cheap creatures that pile onto one target (Dogpile), effects that spread, reveals (doxxing) and clickbait. It pays with exposure (its own creatures get Revealed) and it can't have big creatures.
Mind: Perception and paradox: psychology, cognitive biases, philosophy. It wins without big stats by bending choices: misleading Watchdogs, forced behaviour, rules turned on their head (Attack and Health swapped), fake intents. Its effects are double-edged and its creatures weak: it can't deal raw damage.
Utopia: Science and the future: physics, biology, medicine, space. Its creatures grow for good (gene editing, mutations, exponential growth), the experiments that go too far become abominations, and On the Brink its damage doubles at low Health. It pays with side effects on its own creatures and it can't conceal anything: results get published. Slow, strongest late.

== Time (5)
Day: One turn: both sides plan, then everything resolves in order (moves, summons, spells, then abilities and attacks). It runs from [Dawn] to [Dusk].
Dawn: The start of a [Day], before anyone plans.
Dusk: The end of a [Day], after everything has resolved.
Days: How long it lasts, counting the [Day] it lands: 1 is this Day only, 2 is this Day and the next.
Deployment Time: The [Days] a creature needs to get ready after it's summoned. Until then it can't act and its ability is off. Adding to it only slows a creature still deploying: one already ready isn't sent back.

== Targets (6)
Slot: One of the five places on each side of the table where a creature stands.
Homing: Locks on the enemy creature it's aimed at and follows it wherever it moves (aimed at an enemy slot, other cards hit whatever stands there then). A [Concealed] creature can't be locked on: it hits the slot. [Heat Signature] pulls it: if any creature has Heat, the hottest one on either side takes it, even one of yours.
Random: Picked by chance when the effect happens, among the creatures it can affect at that moment. The one with the most [Heat Signature] is picked first, if any has it. No creature to pick: nothing happens.
Everything: Every creature on both sides, and both players.
Strongest: The creature with the highest Attack, picked when the effect happens. Ties: one of them at random. No creature: nothing happens.
Weakest: The creature with the lowest Attack; tied, the one with the lowest Health. Picked when the card resolves, among your creatures on the board.

== Triggers (7)
Arrival: Happens when this creature lands: once its summon finishes and its [Deployment Time] is over.
Last Will: Happens when this creature dies.
Laid Off: Happens when this card is discarded from your hand: a creature left unplayed at [Dawn], or a card given up to pay a [Discard Cost]. It's your effect, with nothing of it in play: its targets are picked when it happens.
Replaced: Happens when a creature summoned onto its slot replaces it. Being replaced isn't dying: no [Last Will].
Hurt: Happens each time this creature takes damage.
On Hit: Happens when this creature's attack lands, on top of the attack.
When Revealed: Happens when this creature becomes [Revealed], or a [Reveal] shows it to the other side. Against the enemy, it strikes whoever revealed it: the creature whose effect it was, or else its player.

== Keyword Abilities (26)
Counterattack: When a creature attacks this one, this one hits it back for its own Attack. Hitting back isn't an attack, so it's never answered.
Retaliate: Retaliate followed by a status, like [Exhausted|exhaust] or [Burned|Burn] 2: when a creature attacks this one, that status lands on the attacker, with the numbers shown.
Decay: Decay X: takes X damage at each [Dusk].
Regenerate: Regenerate X: heals X at each [Dusk].
Grow: Grow X (no number: 1): +X Attack and +X Health at each [Dawn].
Rebirth: Once per combat, when this would die, it's reborn instead: it survives with 1 Health. The wheel turns: last life a worm, this life a dog.
Summon: Summon X (no number: 1): X random creatures from your deck enter your empty slots.
Destroy: Kills it outright, whatever its Health or [Armor]. [Untouchable] still saves it.
Gone Dark: Off the board until the next [Dawn]: nothing reaches it, and its [Slot|slot] is empty meanwhile (an attack there hits its player). It comes back to its slot, or the first free one, with a new identity: [Concealed] for 2 Days, so the other side doesn't know what it is, and its old statuses are gone. No free slot: it never comes back.
Gamble: Only one of the card's effects happens, picked at random when it resolves. The house always has an edge.
Friendly Fire: Right-drag one of your creatures onto another of yours: it attacks it, a real attack. Its Last Will happens if it dies, which is the point (a Dead Man's Switch, a Martyr, a Cultist).
Send For: Pick a card from your deck (some only look at its top cards, some only take creatures or spells): it joins your next hand, so your plan has what it needs on the Day it needs it. You pick it when you play the card; if you don't, the best one is sent for.
Revive: Once per combat, when this dies, it rises again at the next [Dawn]: its card comes back from your discard pile to the slot where it fell (or the first free one), with full Health. It's ready at once (no [Deployment Time]) and keeps the stat changes it had, but not its statuses. Its Arrival happens again, and its [Last Will] still happens when it dies. No free slot: it stays dead.
Sacrifice: Sacrifice X: an additional cost. When this card resolves, your X [Weakest|weakest] creatures die first; their [Last Will] happens. You can't play it with fewer creatures on the board, and if they're gone by the time it resolves, it fizzles. Myth bends the rules: one offering pays every Sacrifice of its Day, so a creature you sacrificed this Day, or one a Phoenix Feather is taking, pays instead of your weakest.
Prophecy: Prophecy X: the spell is foretold now and comes true at the [Dawn] X [Days] later, aimed where it was played. Prophecy X-Y: at a [Dawn] X to Y [Days] later, at random, and nobody knows which. Everyone hears it: the queue lists what's been foretold.
Single Use: Once played, the card is gone for the rest of the combat instead of going to your discard pile.
Potion: An adventure's: carried from fight to fight (3 at most), in your opening hand, not counted among your spells. Once played it's gone; one not used stays with you.
Gear: A held item: a second ability the creature has while it stays in play, on top of its own. New gear replaces the old; leaving play, it's gone. A [Blackout] switches it off like an ability.
Combo: +1 to the card's numbers (damage, healing, [Armor], cards drawn, stat changes, stacks) for each other spell you play the same [Day], before or after it.
Policy: A spell that becomes a rule of your side: it happens when it resolves, then again at each [Dawn], for the rest of the combat. It doesn't go to your discard pile; the queue lists the Policies in force.
Ripening: A spell that gets better in your hand: at each [Dawn] you keep it, its numbers (damage, healing, [Armor], cards drawn, stat changes, stacks) go up by 1, up to +3. Played or discarded, the next copy starts fresh.
Doom Cost: Doom X, as a cost: when this card resolves, your [Weakest|weakest] creature that isn't [Doomed] yet is Doomed X first: it fights on harder, then dies in X [Days]. You can't play it without one, and if it's gone by the time it resolves, it fizzles. A [Blessed] creature stops the Doom with a stack.
Discard Cost: Discard X: a payment. To play this card, X other cards from your hand (not the ones you're playing) are given up: set aside now, discarded when it resolves, back in your hand if you take the play back. It's paid in full or not at all: with fewer cards, it isn't played and stays in your hand. A calculated card lets you pick them; a chaotic one takes them at random. A card whose effect is to discard (not to pay) discards as many as you have, up to X.
Decoy: A token that isn't what it looks like: until a [Reveal] or [Revealed] shows it, the other side sees the card it pretends to be, with that card's numbers. A token isn't a card in a deck: when it leaves play, it's gone.
Take Control: It changes sides for good: it goes to the [Slot|slot] facing the one it stood in, under your control, and whatever stands there is discarded. It keeps its Health, statuses and stat changes, and loses what it planned. It's still its owner's card: when it leaves play, it goes back to them.
Gift: You play it into an empty enemy slot: it joins their side, and they control it. It's still your card: when it leaves play, it comes back to your discard pile. They can get rid of it by summoning over it.

== Formation (4)
Vanguard: The slot alone at the front of a side. The wall that takes the melee: it can attack only their Vanguard (its attack hits the player when that slot is empty), and it puts on 1 [Armor] each [Dawn], up to 2. See [Reach].
Flanks: The two slots in the middle row. The shooters: +1 Speed while a creature stands there, and they can attack their Vanguard and their Flanks. See [Reach].
Headquarters: The two slots at the back. The important people, hard to reach: no ordinary attack reaches them (only spells, and [Artillery]). From there a creature can attack only their Vanguard. See [Reach].
Reach: Which enemy slots a creature can attack, decided by where it stands: the distance between two slots is how deep each sits ([Vanguard] 0, [Flanks] 1, [Headquarters] 2), added, plus 1. The Vanguard melees at distance 1; the Flanks and Headquarters shoot at 2 to 3; [Artillery] hits at exactly 4. Checked when the attack is planned: moved before it resolves, the creature still hits where it aimed.

== Movement (10)
Leapfrog: Moves by jumping over creatures to an empty slot.
Swap: Trades places with a friendly creature, however far.
Maneuver: A spell that only moves creatures around the board. [Ambivalent] ones work on either side, [Disruption] ones only on the enemy's, [Logistics] ones only on yours. A [Rooted] creature isn't moved.
Ambivalent: A [Maneuver] for either side: reorganise your creatures, or disrupt the enemy's.
Disruption: A [Maneuver] that only moves enemy creatures: chaos in their formation.
Logistics: A [Maneuver] that only moves your own creatures.
Push: It goes one step back: the Vanguard to a Flank (an empty one if there is one), a Flank to the Headquarters slot behind it. A creature already there trades places with it. Headquarters can't go further back.
Pull: It comes one step forward: a Headquarters slot to the Flank in front of it, a Flank to the Vanguard. A creature already there trades places with it. The Vanguard can't come further forward.
Spin: The creatures in the Vanguard and the two Flanks move round one slot, clockwise as that side sees it: the Vanguard's to the right Flank, the right Flank's to the left Flank, the left Flank's to the Vanguard. A [Rooted] creature stays put.
Partner: Its partner in the formation: Flank with Flank, Headquarters with Headquarters; the Vanguard's is the left Flank.

== Stats (3)
Armor: Armor X (no number: 1): absorbs X damage before Health. Fades by 1 each [Dusk].
Health Loss: Lowers Health directly. It isn't damage, so [Armor] doesn't stop it.
Exposed: Takes +1 damage from each hit.

== Statuses (29)
Exhausted: Can't act, its ability is off and it's [Exposed]. Its planned action is cancelled. Lasts the rest of the [Day] and all of the next.
Burned: Burn X: takes X damage at each [Dusk] for the [Days] shown, starting the Dusk after it lands.
Provoked: Provoked X: for the rest of the [Day] it lands and the next X [Days], its attacks go to the creature facing it (or that player, when the slot is empty) whatever it aimed at, and it can't use its Active ability.
Doomed: Doom X (no number: 1): dies in X [Days], with a last attack ([Last Rites]). Meanwhile it gets +1 Attack each [Dawn] and no other status can land on it, except [Borrowed Time]: on Borrowed Time when the sentence is carried out, it survives that too.
Last Rites: A [Doomed] creature's final attack on the slot opposite it, even if it can't act.
Blessed: Bless X (no number: 1): the next X statuses that would land on it are stopped, good or bad. Each stack also saves it once from being [Destroy|destroyed] by a spell or an ability (a Sacrifice isn't stopped).
Heat Signature: Heat Signature X (no number: 1): [Random|random] effects, [Homing] ones and redirected attacks go to the creature with the most Heat Signature, on either side. It stays until cleansed.
Concealed: Conceal X (no number: 1): for X [Days], its plans show as ??? to the enemy, and enemy [Watchdog|Watchdogs] can't catch them.
Revealed: Revealed X (no number: 1): for X [Days], it can't be [Concealed]: its plans can be seen.
Encrypted: Encrypt X (no number: 1): for X [Days], no [Watchdog], the enemy's or yours, can catch its actions. Unlike [Concealed], everyone still sees what it plans.
Untouchable: Untouchable X (no number: 1): takes no damage at all for X [Days], counting the Day it lands: 1 is this Day only.
Cooldown: Cooldown X (no number: 1): its Active ability rests and can't be used for the next X [Days].
Brainwashed: Brainwashed X (no number: 1): when its X [Days] run out, at [Dusk], it changes sides for good ([Take Control|taken] by the other side) and loses its ability until its card is reshuffled. A [Cleanse] can't remove it: only taking it off the board in time (back to a hand, or [Gone Dark|gone dark]) saves it.
Kill Order: Kill Order X (no number: 1): secret, only the side that gave it can see it, and the spell that gives it is played unseen. When its X [Days] run out, at [Dusk], the creature is [Destroy|destroyed]; [Untouchable] still saves it. A [Cleanse] removes it, knowingly or not.
Borrowed Time: Borrowed Time: this Day it can't die, [Lethal] damage or a [Destroy|Destroy]; at its [Dusk], at 0 Health, it's brought back to 1, and Borrowed Time ends.
Lethal: What would kill a creature: damage that takes it to 0 Health, or a [Destroy].
Licensed: Its next attack on a creature [Destroy|destroys] it, whatever its Health or [Armor], and the licence is spent ([Untouchable] or a [Shield] stops the hit, and spends it all the same). It lasts until used. With [Double Tap] both hits carry it: two [Blessed] stacks, or one and the creature.
Survivor: From full Health, a hit that would kill it leaves it at 1 Health instead, and the status is spent. It lasts until used: hurt first, and it doesn't help.
Burnout: Burnout X (no number: 1): when its X [Days] run out, at [Dusk], the creature is [Destroy|destroyed], whatever protects it. Overtime gives it: it lasts through the first Day the rushed creature is ready, so it gets that one Day to act. A [Cleanse] removes it.
Rooted: Rooted X (no number: 1): it can't move, and nothing can move or swap it, for X [Days]. A building going up: while deploying, it stays where it's built.
Composed: Composed X (no number: 1): it can't be [Exhausted] for X [Days], counting the Day it lands (1 is this Day only), and it shakes off a stun it has.
Looping: Groundhog Day: the plan it had as the loop closed (an attack on that slot, a move, an ability, or nothing) is its plan each [Day] it lasts, made for it at [Dawn]; it can plan nothing else, and the plan can't be taken back. A stun still cancels the Day's.
Stalled: It acts last this [Day]: its attack, move or ability comes after every other in its part of the Day.
Deniable: This [Day], effects that hit more than one creature skip it, like [Too Mainstream].
Leeched: Leech X for the [Days] shown: at each [Dusk] after the Day it lands, it takes X and the enemy player heals X.
Mutually Assured: For the [Days] shown: when a creature kills it, that creature is [Destroy|destroyed] too. Destiny Bond.
Blackout: Blackout X (no number: 1): its ability and its effects (arrival, Dawn and Dusk, retaliation, [Last Will]) are switched off for X [Days].
Cleanse: Removes every status, whichever side it helps.
Purge: Removes every status, good and bad.

== Intelligence (8)
Reveal: Shows you everything about a slot: its hidden [Slot Effect], the creature there and what it plans. Instant.
Watchdog: It waits for the actions its card names and catches them as they come: [Disable], [Redirect], [Reorder] or [Reveal]. Once it has caught as many as its card says, it's gone. The enemy sees that you set one, not what it waits for.
Intercept: A [Watchdog] catches an action it watches, before it resolves, and does what it says to it: [Disable], [Redirect], [Reorder] or [Reveal].
Briefing: Each [Day], once everyone has planned and before anything resolves, the [Watchdog] reads the whole queue and [Intercept|intercepts] what it watches. Set during a Day, it looks at what's still to come at once.
Disable: The action is cancelled: it doesn't happen.
Redirect: The action hits another creature instead, picked at [Random|random]. Redirected to its attacker: the attack doesn't happen, and the attacker takes its own Attack as damage.
Reorder: The action moves to the start or the end of the Day.
DMCA: For the [Days] shown, that player can't play a card that shares its name with a creature on the board, either side's: the copy on the board holds the rights.

== Slot Effects (7)
Slot Effect: A condition on a [Slot|slot], not on the creature: it stays with the slot. One per slot.
Landmine: Landmine X: hidden. The next creature to enter the slot takes X damage.
Barbed Snare: Barbed Snare X: hidden. The next creature to leave the slot takes X damage.
Shield: Shield X (no number: 1): for X [Days], counting the one it lands, this creature takes no combat damage (attacks); spells and abilities still hurt it. It goes with the creature. A [Cleanse] doesn't remove it.
Scorched Earth: The creature in the slot can't use its ability or trigger its effects. Its [Arrival] effects wait until it's off the slot. It can still move and attack.
Checkpoint: Checkpoint X: visible, and it stays. Every creature that enters the slot (summoned there, or moved in) takes X damage, for the [Days] shown. Played on a whole side, it makes every summon there cost something.
Prayer: Prayer X: visible. When its [Days] run out, at [Dusk], the creature standing in the slot heals X, whoever it is by then.

== Trends (9)
Grace: A player in Grace (God's Grace) can fall to 0 Health and not lose, until the [Dusk] its Grace ends: still at 0 then, they lose; healed above 0 in time, they live. If both players fall to 0, the one in Grace wins. It doesn't stack: another Grace doesn't put that Dusk off.
Earthquake: It starts at a random slot on either side and zigzags out across the whole board, crossing to the other side at each column: every column is hit once, the sides taking turns, so the creatures facing each other never both take it. Nobody's attack: nothing answers it or redirects it.
Trend: What the world is into right now: a rule for the whole board, for the [Days] shown (no number: until another Trend replaces it). One at a time: a new Trend replaces the old. A Trend card plays on either side of the table. Like the weather, it's the same for both sides: it helps whoever is built for it, and can turn against whoever set it.
Zeitgeist: Each Day, whoever has fewer creatures draws 1 more card.
Rush Hour: The slowest act first. Lower Speed goes first, wherever Speed decides the order.
Survival of the Fittest: Each Dusk, the creature with the least Health on the board, either side, takes 3 damage.
Volcano Lair: Each Day, on each side, the strongest creature hides in a lair: it's Untouchable that Day, as long as another creature of its side stands.
Casino: Each Dawn the house's wheel spins the Day's weather, the same for both sides: Heatwave (every creature +1 Attack), Rain (at Dusk every creature heals 1), Hail (at Dusk every creature takes 1 damage), Tailwind (half the creatures on the board, at random, +1 Speed), Rush Hour (the slowest act first) or Windfall (both players draw 1 more card next Day).
Liminal Space: Every creature's Attack and Health trade places: the ones on the board as it starts, and each one that enters while it trends; they trade back when it ends.
