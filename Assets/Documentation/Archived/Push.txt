PUSH (archived 2026-10-16, the user: "archive the push ability and make it swap on melee range instead")

What it was (GDD 2.4, "Trading Places", every creature, called Push in the code):
A moving creature walked along empty slots up to an occupied slot of its row, from a step away, and traded places with
the creature there, like the bench in Pokemon's card games: the other one took the slot the mover started from. Being
moved wasn't the other creature's own movement, so even a stunned creature was moved. Further than the empty path
allowed, it was blocked ("too far: needs Swap"). The move preview colored those slots orange and labelled them "Push";
the queue said "pushes into".

What replaced it: a swap with the friendly creature one step away (melee range), without walking there first. Further
away needs the Swap keyword. A Rooted creature can't be swapped.

Why kept: if a card ever wants a real push (shoving a creature down the row, maybe an enemy's), this was the rule shape.
