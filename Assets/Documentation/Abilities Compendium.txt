This compendium defines creature abilities that change how stat changes, effects and other abilities work. They are inspired by Pokémon abilities (Contrarian, Mold Breaker, Unaware...). Factions will be assigned once cards are rebalanced.

General Rules:

• One Ability per Creature: A creature has at most one ability. Gaining a new ability (copying, swapping, spreading) replaces the old one.
• Ability Changes Last Until Reshuffle: There are no Day-based or status-based abilities. An ability a creature gains or loses stays on the card, even if it leaves play, until the card is reshuffled into the deck or the combat ends. Then it gets its printed ability back. This is the same rule as Persistent Stat Modifications.
• Abilities Aren't Effects: A creature has at most one ability (from this compendium), but can have many triggered effects ("When this dies, ...", "Each Dawn, ..."). They are different things. Whatever switches things off says which it affects: its ability, its effects, or both.
    • Both: Abilities Disabled (Exhausted, Muted, a Jamming Field) and Deployment Time.
    • Only the ability: Signal Jammer.
    • Both, by standing on it: Scorched Earth (entry effects wait until it's off the slot). The creature can still move and attack.
• Switched Off, Not Removed: Abilities Disabled effects (Muted, Exhausted, Deployment Time, a Jamming Field, Signal Jammer) switch an ability off while they last. The creature still has it.
• Stat Changes vs. Statuses: Abilities that react to stat changes only affect stat modifications (Temporary and Persistent). A status's own stat change (Chill, Heavy, Addicted...) is part of the status and isn't affected, unless the ability says otherwise (Underground does).
• Bouncing Effects: When an ability sends an effect back to "the creature that caused it" and there is no such creature (a Spell from hand, or it has left play), the effect goes to the caster's creature with the lowest current Health, ties broken randomly. If the caster has no creatures, the effect fails. Area effects bounce one creature at a time. An effect can only be bounced once (see GDD 5.2 Status Effects - Bounces and Chains).

Resolution Order for a Stat Change:

When a stat modification targets a creature, it goes through these steps in order:
    1. Bypass: If the source has Zero-Day, the target's defensive abilities are ignored, except Cold Storage.
    2. Block: Unbothered, Cold Storage, Too Mainstream, Cognitive Dissonance and Imposter Syndrome can stop it here.
    3. Reflect: No U deflects it to a random creature on the enemy's side, where it starts again from step 1.
    4. Transform: Contrarian reverses it, then Extremist doubles it.
    5. Apply.
    6. React: Spite triggers.
Underground works at a different moment: when damage is calculated, not when a stat change is applied.

Defensive Abilities (the ones Zero-Day ignores): Contrarian, Extremist, Unbothered, No U, Spite, Underground (when it is attacked), Too Mainstream, Return to Sender, Hive Mind.


Changing How Stat Changes Affect It

Contrarian
    Inspired by: Contrarian (Serperior)
    Type: Passive, Defensive
    Description: "Stat changes on this creature are reversed."
    Notes: +2 becomes -2 and -2 becomes +2; x2 becomes /2 and /2 becomes x2. Applies to stat changes from any source, friendly or enemy.
    Answers: Buff it to hurt it, or simply leave its stats alone. Zero-Day ignores it. Its owner can debuff it on purpose to make it stronger.

Extremist
    Inspired by: Simple (Bibarel)
    Type: Passive, Defensive
    Description: "Stat changes on this creature are doubled."
    Notes: +2 becomes +4 and -1 becomes -2; x2 becomes x4.
    Answers: Every debuff hits twice as hard, so one cheap debuff goes a long way. Its owner gets double value from every buff.

Unbothered
    Inspired by: Clear Body (Metagross)
    Type: Passive, Defensive
    Description: "Enemy effects can't lower this creature's stats."
    Notes: Friendly effects can still lower them, and enemy buffs still apply.
    Answers: Use damage, Health Loss or statuses instead of debuffs, or Zero-Day.

No U
    Inspired by: Mirror Armor (Corviknight)
    Type: Passive, Defensive
    Description: "Enemy stat changes and statuses that land on this creature bounce to a random enemy creature."
    Notes: Every enemy stat change, raises included, and every enemy status. Deflected at random, picked like a Random effect (the one with the most Heat Signature first), never necessarily back to the one that sent it. A deflected effect lands on its sender's side, so it can't bounce again.
    Answers: Debuff it from a creature you don't mind weakening, or use damage instead of debuffs. Zero-Day ignores it.

Spite
    Inspired by: Defiant (Bisharp)
    Type: Passive, Defensive
    Description: "When an effect lowers this creature's stats, friendly or enemy, it gains +2 Attack."
    Notes: The +2 Attack is a Temporary Stat Modification that lasts until it leaves play. It triggers once per stat-lowering effect.
    Answers: Don't debuff it: use damage or statuses. Its owner can debuff it on purpose to grow it.


Ignoring Stat Changes in Combat

Underground
    Inspired by: Unaware (Clefable, Quagsire)
    Type: Passive, Defensive
    Description: "When this creature attacks or is attacked, the other creature's stat changes are ignored."
    Notes: Like Unaware, both ways. When attacked, the attacker hits with its printed Attack plus Persistent Stat Modifications (its buffs and debuffs are ignored). When it attacks, the defender's temporary Health changes are ignored: a Health buff doesn't soak its hit, and a Health debuff doesn't add to it. Its own stat changes still apply.
    Answers: Beat it with base stats and Persistent upgrades instead of temporary buffs. Zero-Day ignores it when Zero-Day attacks it.

Too Mainstream
    Inspired by: original
    Type: Passive, Defensive
    Description: "Immune to effects that affect more than one creature."
    Notes: Area effects, "all creatures" effects, and spreads (Earworm, Burnout's explosion and cleanse spread) don't affect it. Effects that target only this creature still do.
    Answers: Use single-target effects. Zero-Day ignores it.


Breaking Other Abilities

Zero-Day
    Inspired by: Mold Breaker (Haxorus)
    Type: Passive
    Description: "This creature's attacks and effects ignore the target's defensive abilities."
    Notes: Ignores every ability listed under Defensive Abilities. Doesn't bypass Cold Storage. Statuses aren't abilities, so it doesn't ignore them.
    Answers: Cold Storage, Signal Jammer, or switching its ability off (Muted).

Cold Storage
    Inspired by: Full Metal Body (Solgaleo)
    Type: Passive, Defensive
    Description: "Enemy effects can't lower this creature's stats. Zero-Day can't bypass this."
    Notes: The top of the bypass chain. It can still be switched off (Muted, Signal Jammer).
    Answers: Switch its ability off, or use damage, Health Loss or statuses instead of debuffs.

Signal Jammer
    Inspired by: Neutralizing Gas (Weezing), limited to one lane
    Type: Passive, Lane Control
    Description: "The enemy creature directly opposite this creature has its ability switched off."
    Notes: Lasts while both stay in place; moving either creature out of the lane switches the ability back on. It beats Cold Storage, because the ability is switched off, not bypassed.
    Answers: Move your creature out of its lane, or move it out of yours.


Copying and Spreading Abilities

Copypasta
    Inspired by: Trace (Gardevoir)
    Type: Passive
    Description: "When ready, and each Dawn after that while it still has Copypasta, this creature copies the ability of the creature opposite it."
    Notes: Copying replaces Copypasta. If the slot opposite is empty, or that creature has no ability, nothing happens and it tries again next Dawn.
    Answers: Keep the creature opposite it ability-less, or let it copy something that hurts its side (e.g., Contrarian).

Identity Theft
    Inspired by: Skill Swap
    Type: Active
    Description: "Active: swap abilities with target creature."
    Notes: Lasts until each card is reshuffled. The target ends up with Identity Theft and can use it in turn.
    Using It: Active abilities are the creature's action for the Day, instead of attacking or moving (one intent a Day). Hold Shift while dragging the creature onto the target. It's aimed at the slot like an attack, and resolves in the Ability & Attack Layer; if no creature is there by then, it fizzles.
    Answers: Give it nothing worth stealing, or steal back with the Identity Theft it leaves you.

Hive Mind
    Inspired by: Mummy (Cofagrigus)
    Type: Passive, Defensive
    Description: "A creature that attacks it loses its ability and gains Hive Mind instead."
    Notes: The attacker loses its ability until its card is reshuffled. Zero-Day attackers ignore it.
    Answers: Attack it with ability-less creatures, slot attacks from far away, Spells, or Zero-Day.


Other Abilities

Insider Job
    Inspired by: Infiltrator (Crobat)
    Type: Passive
    Description: "This creature's damage ignores Armor."
    Answers: Health and positioning protect against it; Armor doesn't.

Return to Sender
    Inspired by: Magic Bounce (Espeon)
    Type: Passive, Defensive
    Description: "Statuses applied to this creature by enemy effects bounce back to the creature that sent them."
    Notes: A Spell from hand (or a creature no longer in play) bounces to a random creature on the caster's side, picked like a Random effect: the one with the most Heat Signature first (No U deflects every enemy status at random, whoever sent it). A bounced status can't bounce again. Friendly statuses apply normally.
    Answers: Use damage or stat changes instead of statuses, or send the status from a creature that can take it. Zero-Day ignores it.

Undercover
    Inspired by: the GDD's Concealed Actions (2.5 Fog of War & Intelligence)
    Type: Passive
    Description: "This creature's plans show as '???' to the enemy."
    Notes: Everything it plans (attacks, moves, active abilities) is a Concealed Action: the other side sees '???' on its card instead of its arrows, and their Watchdogs can't match it. Revealed cancels it, and a Reveal shows one Day's plans to whoever cast it. It's an ability, so whatever switches abilities off (Exhausted, Signal Jammer...) switches the concealment off too.
    Answers: Revealed, a Reveal, Signal Jammer, or effects that don't care what it plans (slot and area effects).

Ambush
    Inspired by: the GDD's stealthy creatures (2.5, Unknown Creatures): a spy hits hardest before anyone knows who it is. On Multi Passport (it was Sleeper Agent's).
    Type: Passive
    Description: "Enters as ???. Double damage while the enemy doesn't know what it is, or it's Concealed."
    Notes: It's stealthy, like Undercover: it stays '???' (name, stats, art, text) after its summon, though its plans can be seen. Everything it deals is doubled, attacks and effects, to creatures and to the player, while the other side doesn't know what it is or while it's Concealed. A Reveal on its slot or Revealed shows it for good, and the doubling stops; Concealed (Smoke And Mirrors) brings it back while it lasts, unless it's Revealed. Doubled after Penny Stock, before the target's Exposed and Armor. Like any ability, it's off while switched off (Exhausted, Signal Jammer).
    Answers: Reveal it (Eavesdrop, Tapped Communications, Social Panopticon, Control Room), switch its ability off, or take the first hit with Armor.

Extreme Prejudice
    Inspired by: "terminate with extreme prejudice", the spy-world euphemism. Named on purpose not after another game's similar keyword. On Wet Work.
    Type: Passive
    Description: "Its attack destroys the creature it hits, whatever its Health or Armor; then it's gone too."
    Notes: The hit is a Destroy, not damage: Health, Armor, Exposed and damage abilities don't matter. Untouchable or a Shield still stops the hit, and then the assassin survives to try again. Attacking an empty slot, it hits the player for its Attack and stays. One hit, one roadblock: meant for the walls and armored giants nothing else gets through.
    Answers: Kill it before it acts (it waits a Day like every creature), make the target Untouchable, or keep it on a Shield.

Allergic to Light
    Inspired by: vampires and sunlight; for now, spies coming into the light. On If I Told You, I'd Have to Kill You.
    Type: Passive (a drawback)
    Description: "When it's Revealed, or a Reveal shows it, it takes 2 damage."
    Notes: Every time: Revealed landing (Tapped Communications, Social Panopticon, Whistleblower), a Reveal on its slot (Eavesdrop, Control Room), even a Revealed passed to it by Frame Job. It's an ability, so switched off (Exhausted, Signal Jammer) it doesn't burn. Meant for Myth's vampires later.
    Answers: Reveal it. Twice.

Fall Guy
    Inspired by: the patsy who takes the blame, the lone gunman of the official story. On Lone Gunman (was Scapegoat).
    Type: Passive
    Description: "When an enemy effect would make another friendly creature Revealed or destroy it, it lands on this one instead."
    Notes: Only enemy effects, and only Revealed (not a Reveal's look) and Destroy (Perfectly Timed Camera Malfunction, Fell Out of a Window, Extreme Prejudice, Dead Man's Switch): damage still goes where it was aimed. The leftmost Fall Guy on the side takes it. A Kill Order is secret, so nobody takes the fall for it.
    Answers: Kill the Lone Gunman first (it has 3 Health), or switch its ability off.

Handover
    Inspired by: Baton Pass (Pokémon), renamed so it isn't the move's name
    Type: Passive
    Description: "When a summon replaces it, the newcomer takes over its stat changes and statuses."
    Notes: Everything that makes it differ from its printed card moves to the creature summoned onto its slot: Persistent Stat Modifications, timed and lasting stat changes, and every status, good or bad (a Doom included). Its own card goes to the discard pile back to its printed stats. Like any ability, it does nothing while switched off (not ready, Exhausted, jammed).
    Answers: Kill it before it's replaced, cleanse what it carries, or switch its ability off.

Recoil
    Inspired by: Recoil (Pokémon's Double-Edge, Brave Bird)
    Type: Passive
    Description: "+2 damage on its attacks; it takes 1 damage after each attack."
    Notes: The +2 is part of the attack (Penny Stock then sees the bigger number), against a creature or a player. The 1 damage comes after the hit, with no attacker, so nothing answers it.
    Answers: Make it attack less, or let it wear itself down.

Penny Stock
    Inspired by: Technician (Scizor), renamed: a mechanic from another game doesn't keep that game's name (see GDD 6.1, Naming). On Pluriemployed Hustler.
    Type: Passive
    Description: "Double damage when it hits for 3 or less."
    Notes: Checked after the attacker's own stat calculation, before the target's modifiers (Exposed, Cognitive Dissonance) and Armor. 0 damage stays 0.
    Answers: Armor and damage reduction still blunt it. Buffing its hit above 3 actually switches the doubling off.

Collateral Damage
    Inspired by: Trample (Magic: The Gathering). First called Trickle Down; renamed by the user (2026-10-15). On C-Suite (Corporate).
    Type: Passive
    Description: "When its attack is more than a creature's Health, the rest hits that creature's player."
    Notes: Only its attacks (combat damage, Double Tap and Spray and Pray hits included), not its effects. What's left is
           the hit after Armor and the creature's Health: 7 against 1 Armor and 4 Health puts 2 on the player. A hit that
           something stops (Untouchable, a Shield) has nothing left to pass on. It's combat damage to the player.
    Answers: Big creatures, Armor and Health buffs soak it; Untouchable stops it all.

Protecting, Copying and Moving Other Creatures

Each of these also exists as an effect any card can carry (a spell, or a creature's trigger), so a card can do it without the ability: Untouchable (a status), copy stats, steal stat raises, swap places, and gain an ability (any ability below, or above, given by a spell or trigger, replacing the creature's own until its card is reshuffled). On a spell, the two-creature effects work between the target and the creature facing it.

Bodyguard
    Inspired by: Protect (Pokémon), given to another creature
    Type: Active (no Shift needed)
    Description: "Can't attack. Drag it onto another friendly creature: that one is Untouchable this Day. Then a Day of Cooldown."
    Notes: It never attacks, even while its ability is off (it's printed on it). Its guard lands with the spells, before the Day's attacks, as help played this Day does (GDD 3.7), so it stops faster attackers too. Untouchable stops all damage, combat or not (Burn included). Cooldown 1 rests it the next Day: it guards every other Day. A Bless on the Bodyguard negates its own Cooldown.
    Answers: Kill or Exhaust the Bodyguard, cleanse Untouchable, or hit with Health Loss and statuses (they aren't damage).

Wannabe
    Inspired by: Transform (Pokémon), stats only
    Type: Active
    Description: "Its Attack, Health and Speed become target creature's."
    Notes: A copy, damage included: its maximum and current Health become the target's. It lasts while it stays in play, like a lasting stat change, but abilities that bend stat changes (Contrarian, Unbothered...) don't touch it. Its own ability stays.
    Answers: Copy it while weak, or kill what it would copy first.

Credit Grab
    Inspired by: Spectral Thief (Pokémon)
    Type: Active, once per combat
    Description: "Steals target creature's stat raises."
    Notes: Every raise moves over as it is: lasting and timed ones (with their Days left), and the raises in its Persistent Stat Modifications (those stay on the thief's card). Lowered stats stay on the victim. A victim held up by a Health raise can die of it.
    Answers: Buff after it has used it, or keep buffs on creatures it can't reach.

Spray and Pray
    Inspired by: spread moves (Pokémon's Earthquake, Surf), aimed
    Type: Passive
    Description: "Its attacks also hit the creatures beside its target."
    Notes: A cone of three slots on the other side: the target slot and its two neighbors, each hit for its Attack as combat damage (each hit can be answered). An empty target slot still hits the player, and the neighbors too. Too Mainstream creatures beside the target are skipped.
    Answers: Spread out, or put Too Mainstream creatures beside the one it aims at.

Chain Mail
    Inspired by: chain lightning
    Type: Passive
    Description: "Its attacks jump to the next 2 creatures in line, half the damage each jump."
    Notes: It jumps toward the side with more creatures in a row, from the creature it hit: 4 Attack hits for 4, then 2, then 1. Only from a creature: hitting a player doesn't chain.
    Answers: Leave gaps in your line.

Double Tap
    Inspired by: two-hit moves (Pokémon's Double Kick)
    Type: Passive
    Description: "It attacks twice."
    Notes: A second hit on the same target right after the first, each for its Attack as it is then. Armor, Counterattack and Retaliate see two hits. Recoil costs it 1 per attack, not per hit.
    Answers: Armor, Untouchable, and creatures that answer every hit.

Shell Game
    Inspired by: Ally Switch (Pokémon), on anyone
    Type: Active
    Description: "Target creature trades places with the one to its right."
    Notes: To its left at the end of the row; an empty slot just moves it. It isn't a move (nobody chose to move), so nothing stops it, but Slot Effects see both creatures leave and enter. Attacks aim at slots, so it can put an enemy into a planned attack, or a friend out of one.
    Answers: Plan after you see it, or keep Slot Effects off your neighbours' slots.

Safe Space
    Inspired by: Safeguard (Pokémon)
    Type: Passive
    Description: "Each Dawn, it and the friendly creatures beside it gain Bless."
    Notes: Bless 1 each Dawn, a ward that stops the next status, good or bad (Blessed stacks up to 3). Nothing at the Dawn it lands: it starts the Dawn after it's ready.
    Answers: Spend the wards with weak statuses first, or use damage.
