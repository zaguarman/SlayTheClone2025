FACTION BALANCE PLAN (2026-10-16)

The goal: the nine factions within reach of each other, judged as factions, not deck by deck (the user). A faction is
judged two ways: its best deck (what it can do built well) and the average of all its decks (how forgiving its card pool
is). Two-faction decks are listed apart.


1. PRINCIPLES

• Buff the weak up to the strong; no nerfs unless the user asks (mild ones were accepted before, when asked).
• Keep each faction's rhythm (Faction Compendium, "Every Faction Has a Rhythm"): Corporate and Shadow Ops. are timing
  factions (they keep their speed; their numbers move), Statecraft and Myth hold the status quo (durability, protection).
• Change what the faction lacks, not one deck's list: a hole in the card pool is filled with new cards; a mechanic that
  loses everywhere is fixed as a mechanic; weak bodies get stats.
• Measure after every round, the same way: the AI Balance Report (Very Hard AI, every deck against every other, mirror
  games for the cards) and the Adventure Simulation (30 runs a faction).


2. WHAT THE DATA SAID (the tournament under the new hand rules, before any change)

• Factions (all their decks): Digital Society 75%, Mind 71, Statecraft 69, Tech 66, Zeitgeist 49, Myth 45, Corporate 28,
  Utopia 28, Shadow Ops. 25.
• What wins: Unbothered, Armor, Watchdogs, Send For, Leapfrog, Exhaust, Heat Signature, board damage, big bodies.
• What loses: tokens (10%), Swap, Doomed, Blessed, Burn, Last Will, conditional Destroy, fast small creatures, draw.
• Strategies: Midrange and Swarm win, Control holds, Aggro, Combo and Engine lose.
• The holes: Shadow Ops. had no plain damage (its starter deck could not finish a 5-Health enemy) and no walls;
  Utopia's bodies hurt themselves and its removal hurt its player; Corporate's creatures were 1/1s to 2/3s and its
  numbers had been cut on 2026-10-14; Myth's knights were slow and soft; Zeitgeist's swarm had nothing to make it stick.


3. THE LEVERS, IN ORDER

1) New cards for the holes (30, 2026-10-16): Shadow Ops. 7 (Exploding Pen, Drug Pigeon, Embassy, Secret Anthrax
   Laboratory, Tracking Beacon, Arsonist, Counter-Surveillance), Utopia 6 (Longevity Hub, Femtosecond Laser, Quark,
   Seasteading, Ankylorhiza, White Dwarf), Corporate 6 (Venture Capital, Corporate Bailouts, Flash Crash, Just in Time,
   Hedonic Treadmillionaire, Moral Bankruptcy), Myth 6 (Grim Reaper, Blessing in Disguise, Die by the Sword, Mithril,
   Here Be Dragons, Gracias Jesús), Zeitgeist 5 (Listen Kid, Ol' Reliable, Weaponized Incompetence, Absentee Parents,
   The Friends We Made Along the Way). Each faction's two decks took the ones they needed most.
2) Stats for the weakest bodies (+1 here and there) and the 2026-10-14 cuts given back: Corporate (Margin Call 2,
   Hostile Takeover 4, its small creatures +1, its big ones a Day faster), Shadow Ops. (Femme Fatale, Cleaner, MK Ultra,
   If I Told You back up, its walls and spies +1), Utopia (slow science a Day sooner, Chimera hurts itself less),
   Myth (sturdier faithful).
3) Next, if the gap stays: the mechanics that lose everywhere. Blessed (a stack could also stop 1 damage), Doomed on an
   enemy (it gets +1 Attack a Dawn: a curse that helps them early), Burn on your own creatures (Utopia's cost), Last Will,
   tokens. Fixing a mechanic lifts every faction that owns it at once.
4) Last, and only if asked: mild trims at the top (Zeitgeist's Moods of Modern Life, Digital Society's Terminally Online).

5. NEXT

• Utopia and Corporate: lever 3 (Burn on their own creatures, Engine's draw) before more stats.
• The second decks of a faction are first drafts (Black Budget, Hustle Rush, Lab Grown, Blessed Martyrs, Underdogs): they
  drag the all-decks average down; rebuild them from the card stats before judging a faction on them.
• The report doesn't credit damage that lands later (Dead Cat Bounce's crash, Prophecies like Inflation) to its card: their
  impact reads 0. The effects work (checked); the ledger needs to follow the delayed actions to their card.
• Adventures: the elites (Hard AI, a stage more Health) end most runs; Myth and Corporate never beat the boss.


6. THE META PICKS (2026-10-16, the user: "implement all of your ideas", from the Pokémon and Slay the Spire report)

• New rules: Ripening (spells that grow in the hand), Laid Off (a card discarded from the hand does something), Toxic
  (Burn that grows) with Hormesis (heals from it), Martyrdom (Doomed doubles it), Grudge (+1 Attack per friendly death),
  side hazards (Checkpoint on a whole side) with a sweeper and Projection, Sucker Punch (priority), Hit and Run (pivot),
  Policies (spells that repeat each Dawn), Shield Bash (Armor as Attack), Strip Search (purge and hit), On Trend.
• 29 cards to carry them, names from the Brainstorm List, in the decks they fit (Workbench: the showcase).
