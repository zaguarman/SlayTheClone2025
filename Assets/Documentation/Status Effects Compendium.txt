Status Timing (GDD 5.2 Status Effects - Status Timing): Every status skips the Day it's applied. Unless an entry says otherwise, its stacks are Days: applied on Day X, it first acts and loses a stack at the Dusk of Day X+1 (or the Dawn of Day X+2 for a Dawn status). Each tick, its effect happens first, then it loses 1 stack. When the last stack goes, it ends and does whatever its entry says (evolve, spread, pay off).

Cleansing: Every status is cleansable unless its entry says "Cleansable: No". Cleansing an uncleansable status has no effect, unless the entry defines what a cleanse attempt does instead (like Burnout). A Cleanse only removes harmful statuses; a Purge removes positive ones too (GDD Cleanse). Positive statuses so far: Blessed.

Stat Changes: A status's stat change applies only while the status is on the creature. It is independent from Temporary and Persistent Stat Modifications (see GDD 3.3 Creatures & Action Types). Stats can drop to 0, never below.

Status Ownership: Every status belongs to one faction, or is Neutral. A faction's cards apply its own statuses freely; other factions apply them only rarely and at a higher cost (see 'Faction Compendium'). Each faction owns at most 3 statuses.

• Neutral: Anchored, Concealed, Exposed, Muted, Revealed
• Myth: Blessed, Bloodthirst, Doomed
• Statecraft: Diplomatic Immunity, Heavy, Keep Calm
• Shadow Ops.: Brainwashed, Compromised, Honeypot

Doomed
    Faction: Myth
    Type: Delayed, Unique, Damage, Statistic, Fixed_Stacks
    Fixed_Stacks: Yes
    Description: "Death Sentence. Removes other statuses and blocks new ones. Each Dawn, gains 1 permanent Attack. When Consumed: Last Rites, then destroyed. Can't be cleansed, only passed on."
    Core Impact: A guaranteed death, usable as a weapon or as a calculated sacrifice. The creature grows stronger until it falls and strikes on its way out.
    Stacking & Duration:
        Initial Stacks: X (from source)
        Re-application Behavior: Ignore
        On Consumed: Last Rites, then this creature is destroyed. Both happen immediately, as part of the status being consumed.
    Statistic Modification:
        Target Statistic: Attack
        Modification Method: +1 (cumulative, Each Dawn)
        Modification Scope: Permanent (a Persistent Stat Modification: it stays on the card, so a revived creature keeps it)
    Turn-Based Effects:
        Trigger: Each Dawn
        Effect: Apply the linked Statistic Modification (+1 Attack).
    Last Rites: The creature makes one final Attack against the slot opposite it, using its current Attack. It resolves even if the creature Cannot Act.
    Notes:
        • Upon application, all other status effects (positive and negative, regardless of cleansability) are removed from this creature, and it is immune to gaining new ones while Doomed.
        • Blessed is the only thing that can stop Doomed from being applied. Once it's applied, the countdown can't be changed, delayed or cured by any effect; the sentence can only be passed on (see Heresy).
        • Passing it onto a Blessed creature uses up a Blessed stack and the sentence is negated: a scarce, expensive way out.
        • As a weapon: dooming an enemy guarantees it dies, but it grows every Dawn and strikes back with Last Rites.
        • As a sacrifice: dooming your own creature gives a growing attacker with a parting blow, and the Attack it gained stays on the card.
    Answers: Pass it to a vessel you meant to sacrifice anyway (Heresy), use the Attack it gains, and aim Last Rites at the enemy's best slot.
    Cleansable: No. Heresy: a cleanse attempt passes Doomed on instead of removing it (GDD 5.2 Status Effects - Passing a Status). It moves, with its remaining stacks, to the creature with the lowest current Health on this creature's side; that vessel loses its other statuses as usual. This creature is freed and keeps the Attack it gained. If it has no other creature on its side, the sentence is carried out immediately: Last Rites, then it is destroyed. If that vessel is Doomed already, Heresy fizzles: nothing happens, and this creature stays Doomed.

Heat Signature
    Faction: Tech
    Type: Unique, Control, Stacking, Persistent
    Persistent: Yes
    Description: "Random effects and redirected attacks go to the creature with the most Heat Signature."
    Core Impact: A cold war: both sides pile Heat on creatures to steer the other side's Homing attacks.
    Stacking & Duration:
        Intensity: Heat stacks, X from the source ("gains X Heat"). They never decay; only cleansing removes them.
        Re-application Behavior: Add Stacks
        Max Stacks: None for now (99 is only the display limit; a real cap will come with balancing).
    Notes:
        • What it catches: every effect with a randomly chosen target, of any kind (damage, statuses, stat changes, Reveal...), and every action a Watchdog redirects, from either player, including the controller's own. A random effect picks among the creatures it can affect ("a random enemy creature" among the enemy's), so Heat decides which of them; a redirected action can go to any creature. Attacks aimed at a slot (Fixed, Static) aren't affected. Future: Homing attacks too.
        • When: like a Watchdog, just before each such attack resolves, so it also pulls attacks that were queued before it was applied.
        • Which one: the creature with the most Heat, on either side. If several are tied for the most, the nearest one: first in the attacker's own row, counted in slots; only if there is none in its row, in the other row, counted by column difference. Remaining ties are broken randomly.
        • Example with equal Heat (columns 1-5): row 1 is A, X, H1, c, X and row 2 is Y, Y, B, C, H2. A's Homing attack lands on H1 (its own row). B's lands on H2 (its own row), even though H1 is directly opposite. C's attack aimed at the slot of c isn't affected. If H1 had more Heat than H2, both attacks would land on H1.
    Answers: Out-heat the enemy: keep your armored tank as the hottest creature and it soaks every Homing attack, including your own, so switch to slot attacks. Heat an enemy creature above everything else and both sides' Homing attacks hit it. Cleansing resets the race.
    Cleansable: Yes
