1. PURPOSE

• Three monofaction decks, one per faction: Statecraft has 20 different cards, Corporate 18, Zeitgeist 17. No copies yet.
• Every card name comes from the 'Brainstorm List' or the 'Refined Brainstorm List', picking the most cynical ones. Margin Call is the exception: a Corporate staple added on purpose.
• Cards only use what the engine already does: damage, healing, armor, draws, summoning from the deck, stat changes (ModifyStat), the Exhausted stun, and, for the cards in section 6, Doomed, Burn, Health Loss, Leapfrog and Swap.
• Priority (Action) is used by Preemptive Strike, Slot Effects by Lost Landmine and Ceasefire. No Watchdogs, control changes or Locations yet.
• No card checks or rewards the presence of another faction (no "deals more damage to Corporate creatures").
• Every card is a placeholder for a future GDD version. That version is stored on the card asset itself (the "Design Notes" field in the Inspector) and summarized below as "Future".

Where things live
    • Cards: Assets/Scripts/Generators/FactionDecksGenerator.cs (menu: Cards > Generate Faction Decks). Running it again updates the cards in place, keeps their ids and art, and deletes card assets no longer in the list.
    • Art: Tools/generate_card_art.py, then Cards > Assign Card Art. Corporate uses an office skyline with a stock line, Statecraft a propaganda poster, Zeitgeist a vaporwave sunset.
    • Decks: Assets/Scriptables/Decks/CorporateDeck, StatecraftDeck, ZeitgeistDeck, and the MechanicsTestDeck (section 6).
    • Pictures: each card asset has an "Art Description" field with the brief for its picture.
    • Main menu: Assets/Scriptables/Decks/DeckCatalog lists the decks you can pick and one opponent per deck. Play opens the match setup: your deck, your opponent, and their deck (they bring their own unless you swap it).
    • Opening the game scene directly plays Corporate against Statecraft (the decks set on GameReferences).


2. HOW THE PLACEHOLDERS MAP TO THE GDD

Faction costs (Faction Compendium, "Pays With")
    • Corporate (Capital, discard cards): Discarding as a cost doesn't exist yet. Some cards give the enemy a card or a buff instead (Day Trader, Hostile Takeover, Multimillionaire Philanthropist), or hurt you later (Charles Ponzi).
    • Statecraft (Bureaucracy): Implemented as is. Its creatures take 1–2 turns to deploy, and Martial Law stuns your own side.
    • Zeitgeist (Self-harm): Implemented as is. Damage to its own creatures and to you, and chaos that hits both sides.

Faction holes (Faction Compendium, "Can't")
    • Corporate never heals. Statecraft never hides anything (nothing hides yet). Zeitgeist has no Intelligence (nothing reveals yet).

Mechanic stand-ins
    • Red Tape (+Deployment Time) -> Stun 1 turn.
    • Priority strikes -> high Speed.
    • Recruitment -> summon random creatures from your own deck.
    • Investments / Persistent Stat Modifications -> ModifyStat, which lasts while the creature stays on the battlefield.
    • Burnout / Coffeinated -> "At the end of each turn, deal 1 damage to this."
    • Strikes -> stun every creature on both sides.
    • OnDeploymentTimeComplete -> "When this enters" on a creature with Deployment Time, which already fires when it becomes operative.

6. MECHANICS TEST CARDS & DECK

Cards made on 2026-10-12 to try the mechanics built that day: movement (GDD 2.4), Exhausted, Doomed, Health Loss, Armor, Homing and Fixed aim (4.6), the Creature Death Sequence (3.9), Status Timing (5.2) and Deployment Time (3.2). Each card's Design Notes say what it tests; its Art Description holds the brief for its picture.
• Corporate, Statecraft and Zeitgeist cards joined their faction decks.
• Digital Society, Shadow Ops., Tech, Myth and Mind have no deck yet (a deck needs 12 cards). Their cards wait in Assets/Scriptables/Cards/<Faction>/ for those decks.
• The Mechanics Test deck mixes them with Preemptive Strike (Priority), 20 in all (Inflation stays out: Generational Trauma already tests Health Loss). It's a test deck: the DeckCatalog offers it next to the faction decks, but no opponent brings it and the one-faction rule doesn't apply.

Creatures (Attack/Health/Speed, Deployment Time)
    • Cleaning Robot Premium (Corporate, 2/7/1, 1): no ability. Tests pushing, blocked pushes and the automatic move order.
    • Diplomat (Statecraft, 3/5/1, 2): When ready, each friendly creature gains 2 Armor. Tests Deployment Time at Dusk and Armor fading.
    • Delivery Cyclist (Zeitgeist, 1/3/3, 0): Leapfrog.
    • Digital Nomad (Digital Society, 2/2/2, 0): Leapfrog.
    • Keyboard Warrior (Digital Society, 1/3/2, 0): Whenever this is damaged, 1 damage to whoever damaged it. Tests OnDamaged on every hit, even killing ones.
    • Political Decoy (Shadow Ops., 0/6/1, 1): Swap.
    • Martyr (Myth, 2/4/1, 1): When ready, doom this for 2 Days. When it dies, heal each friendly creature by 3.

Spells
    • Inflation (Corporate): Each enemy creature gets -1 Health (Health Loss).
    • Raised Security Level (Statecraft): Target friendly creature gains 6 Armor. Tests the Armor cap and Homing.
    • Dogpile (Digital Society): 1 damage to an enemy creature and to the creatures next to it.
    • Rage Bait (Digital Society): Burn an enemy creature for 2 each Dusk for 2 Days, starting the next Day.
    • DDOS Attack (Tech): Exhaust (stun) an enemy creature for 1 Day.
    • Memento Mori (Myth): Doom a creature for 3 Days.
    • Generational Trauma (Mind): An enemy creature gets -2 Health, which stays on the card.
    • The Holy Grail (Myth): Bless a friendly creature twice (Blessed 2: each stack negates the next status that would land).
    • Exorcism (Myth): Cleanse a creature's statuses. On a Doomed creature it's Heresy: the sentence passes to the weakest creature on its side.
    • Lost Landmine (Statecraft): A hidden Landmine (3 damage) on a slot: the first creature to enter it takes the damage, before anything else happens.
    • Ceasefire (Statecraft): A Temporal Shield on a slot for 2 Days: the creature there takes no combat damage.
    • Its A Trap (Digital Society): A hidden Barbed Snare (2 damage) on a slot: the first creature to leave it, even pushed or swapped, takes the damage.
    • Burn Notice (Shadow Ops.): Scorched Earth on a slot: the passive abilities of the creature there don't trigger.

Waiting on a decision
    • Plot Armor (Zeitgeist, 2/2/2, 0): survives a lethal hit once per combat. Cards can't say "once per combat" yet, so it isn't made.


7. NEXT STEPS

• Balance is untested. Start by playing each deck against the others from the main menu.
• There is no computer opponent yet: both sides are played by you on the same screen.
• Known bug #45 (two summons from the deck in one turn can land in the same slot) can affect Multilevel Marketing Seller.
• When a GDD mechanic lands, update the matching cards in FactionDecksGenerator.cs and clear their Design Notes.
• Names left out on purpose: brainstorm entries that mock groups of people rather than institutions or behaviours.
