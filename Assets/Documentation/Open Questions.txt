    • For now its creature's entry effects wait until it's off the slot. The mechanic as a whole is to be reworked later; ideas welcome.


ANSWERED (2026-10-13, round 13)

• Scorched Earth: an entry effect it stops waits, and happens once the creature is off the scorched slot (it moved, or the Scorched Earth was cleansed or ran out). Once only. The whole mechanic will be reworked later (4b).
• Encrypted belongs to the factions that use computers: Shadow Ops., Digital Society, Corporate, Statecraft and Zeitgeist. Concealed and Encrypted are good statuses (a Cleanse leaves them).
• Aimed spells can hit your own creatures on purpose (to feed abilities that react to damage, statuses...): no change.
• Counterattack and Retaliate don't answer spells: as they are.
• Placebo: +3 Health for good: as it is.
• Heat Signature: random damage is drawn from both sides again (see 29).
• Ability test creatures: they work; more will come.
• Decks: Digital Society, Shadow Ops., Tech, Myth and Mind each have a deck of their own (20 cards, some twice). Only two example pairs stay: Digital Society & Shadow Ops. (Panopticon: Heat Signature and Reveal) and Myth & Corporate (Prosperity Gospel).
• Names: "Involuntary Free Polonium Giveaway" (spelling fixed). Second Wind is now Rebirth.
• Handover: off while Exhausted; when it's on, it passes everything, good and bad. As it is.
• Names too close to someone else's: Handover and Penny Stock stay renamed (another game's mechanics); Fat Tony is back (a real person).
• Art: pictures stay as they are unless you ask for a change (quirky is fine). Redrawn on request: No Glove, No Love (a woman saying no, no; its old knight picture went to the new Sex Clanker), Meta Group Chat, Social Panopticon, Control Room, Humanitarian Trafficking Action, No Glove, No Love, Scope Creep, Scapegoating (the escape goat), Ship of Theseus.
• The new building blocks (Bodyguard, Wannabe, Credit Grab...): as they are.
• No U: bounces stat changes and statuses, deflected at random (see 29).
• Move ghosts show "Moving" instead of "Queued".
• The Queue panel: fine where it is.
• The note under End Turn is always there: the fog's state (F9), F8 to play as player 2, Esc for the menu, Shift + drag for abilities.
• Deck builder: 20 to 40 cards, 2 copies at most. Starter decks are listed in the builder to look at and copy. Once-per-combat stays per card name: two copies share the one use.
• Numbers: Grow and Armor drop their 1 too.
• Keywords screen: a filter above the list finds keywords by name.
• Spell ghosts: the edge is right, and the sizes. Several spells at one place (up to 4): the newest in the middle, the older ones stepping left; hover to see, click to take back.
• Meta: a plain word, the trademark doesn't matter.

ANSWERED (2026-10-12)

• Fog of War views: the rules know everything; each player sees their own cards and what their cards revealed. The enemy's hand shows card backs and the enemy's cards can't be used. The screen plays player 1's side (Debug > Play As Player 2 / F8 switches, for testing).
• Unknown cards: minimalist, a normal card layout left empty on black, with three bold question marks in the middle; the enemy hand shows a card back with a keyhole seal.

• Scorched Earth: stops the creature's triggered effects. Its ability is switched off too.
• Barbed Snare: only moving out counts as leaving; dying on the slot or being replaced doesn't.
• Watchdog conditions: each Watchdog card gets its own filter (ad hoc); shared templates may come later for filters that repeat. The number filter stays an attack's Attack for now.
• Abilities on cards: Day Trader (Insider Job), Pluriemployed Hustler (Penny Stock), Spite-Elected President (Spite), Supreme Court (Return to Sender), Hipster (Too Mainstream), Keyboard Warrior (No U); ten new test creatures carry the rest.
• Pictures: Its A Trap (Homer and the free-boat sting), Burn Notice (the Banksy self-shredding painting), Antivirus (a panda), Counter-Intelligence (the first Counter-Strike cover with accountants and calculators). Red Tape, YOLO and Preemptive Strike got briefs.

• Action Priority: removed; order inside a layer is Speed. Red Tape now adds Deployment Time, YOLO gives +4 Attack for this Day, Preemptive Strike is plain damage. Docs use Speed where they used Priority.
• Cleanse / Purge: Cleanse removes only what harms (bad statuses, stat decreases); Purge removes good and bad. Mixed statuses count as bad.
• Heresy: if the vessel is Doomed already, it fizzles and the creature stays Doomed.
• Moves: only the slot a creature lands on counts as entered. Planned moves show ghost cards where creatures will end up.
• Hidden effects in hot-seat: player 1 (the screen) sees only their own.
• Timed Slot Effects: "2 Days" is this Day and the next (used to stall).
• Abilities vs. effects: separate things; one ability, many triggered effects; what switches them off says which (Abilities Compendium).
• Identity Theft: the creature's action for the Day; hold Shift while dragging it onto the target.
• Underground: like Unaware, both ways.
• Watchdog Redirect: to a random creature; Heat Signature draws it first (most Heat).
• Watchdog charges: one per action it changes.
• Plot Armor: made, with a "once per combat" rule that lasts through reshuffles.
• Waiting for other systems: Watchdog Create, an AI opponent, the remaining Slot Effects (Revealed, Anchored, Banish...).
