Open Questions

5. Fog of War: what's done and what's still open
    • Done: Concealed, Revealed and Encrypted statuses, the Undercover ability, Reveal (instant), Watchdogs blind to Concealed actions and ignoring Encrypted ones, the '???' on screen, and a debug switch (Debug > Fog of War, F9 in play).

6. New decks (2026-10-13): own decks or pairs?
    • Done: Utopia had no cards, so it got its own deck of 20. Digital Society, Shadow Ops., Tech, Myth and Mind now have 8 to 12 cards each; they stay in the three two-faction decks, which grew to 20 cards each (42 new cards in all, names from the Brainstorm Lists).
    • Question: should each of those five get a deck of its own (about 8 to 12 more cards each), or are the pairs the way to go?

7. Aimed spells can be dropped on either side
    • Nothing says "target friendly creature" or "target enemy creature" yet, so a spell like Gene Editing can be aimed at an enemy too. Sacrificial Ritual was made to pick a random friendly creature so it can't be thrown at the enemy for 5 damage.
    • Question: should aimed spells be limited to the side their text names (friendly or enemy)?

8. Retaliation text
    • An enemy-targeted "Whenever this is damaged" effect hits whoever damaged it (rules, since earlier); the card text now says "whoever damaged it" instead of "the enemy player" (Keyboard Warrior, Quantum Physicist). If a spell damages it, there's no attacker: damage then goes to the enemy player, and a stun does nothing.
    • Since 2026-10-13: these are Counterattack and Retaliate, and they answer attacks only. A spell or an ability that damages the creature isn't answered at all, and hitting back isn't an attack, so two Counterattack creatures never trade blows forever.
    • Question: OK, or should spell damage count the spell's caster as the one who damaged it?

9. The Queue panel
    • A Queue button at the right edge of the table, level with your mat; the panel fills the corner above End Turn. No hotkey.
    • Question: OK where it is, and want a hotkey for it?

13. Heat Signature and random effects (2026-10-13)
    • Done: every random effect (not only damage) goes to the creature with the most Heat, among the creatures it can affect: "a random friendly creature +1 Attack" goes to your hottest creature, "a random enemy creature" to the enemy's hottest.
    • Changed: random damage used to look for Heat on both sides, so your own hot creature could catch your own "random enemy" damage. Now it only picks among the creatures the effect can reach. Redirected attacks (Watchdog) still go to the hottest creature on either side.
    • Question: OK, or should random damage keep pulling from both sides?

14. Deck builder rules (2026-10-13)
    • Done: your own decks in the main menu (Decks), from any cards on offer: 20 cards (a starter deck's size), at most 2 copies of each. Only complete decks can be played; either side can play them. A new deck starts empty.
    • Question: are 20 cards and 2 copies right? Should a new deck be able to start as a copy of a starter deck?
    • Question: with copies in a deck, "once per combat" (Plot Armor, Second Wind) is tracked by the card, so two copies share one use. Should each copy get its own?

15. Numbers that go without saying (2026-10-13)

16. Counterattack and Retaliate answer attacks only (2026-10-13)
    • Done: Counterattack hits back for the creature's Attack; Retaliate puts its status on the attacker. Both answer a creature's attack that lands (Armor included). A spell or an ability that damages the creature isn't answered, and hitting back isn't an attack, so two of them never trade blows forever.
    • Changed: Keyboard Warrior went from Retaliate 1 to Counterattack (same 1 damage, its Attack). A fixed-damage answer is still possible in the data and reads "Retaliate: 1 damage to it".
    • Question: should spells and abilities that damage it be answered too?

22. The new building blocks (2026-10-13)
    • Done: each mechanic works as an ability, a trigger or a spell. Abilities: Bodyguard (protect), Wannabe (copy stats), Credit Grab (steal stat raises, once per combat), Spray and Pray (cone of three), Chain Mail (chained hits), Double Tap (attacks twice), Shell Game (switch places), Safe Space (status wards). Effects any card can use: the statuses Untouchable and Cooldown, copy stats, steal stat raises, swap places, gain an ability. One card each, in the Mechanics Test deck and the deck builder.
    • Choices made, to check:
        • Bodyguard's protection lasts this Day only (Untouchable 1) and lands before the attacks; with its Day of rest it guards every other Day. It can't attack even when its ability is off.
        • Swapping moves the target with the creature to its right (left at the end of the row), not a pick of two creatures.
        • The copy includes damage (its Health becomes the target's), and lasts while it stays in play.
        • Credit Grab takes only raises (lowered stats stay), including raises for good, and statuses stay.
        • Chain Mail halves from the first jump (4, then 2, then 1).
        • "Ward" is Bless: the ward that stops the next status. Safe Space gives Bless 1 at each Dawn to itself and its neighbors.
    • Question: any of these to change?

ANSWERED (2026-10-12)

• Fog of War views: the rules know everything; each player sees their own cards and what their cards revealed. The enemy's hand shows card backs and the enemy's cards can't be used. The screen plays player 1's side (Debug > Play As Player 2 / F8 switches, for testing).
• Unknown cards: minimalist, a normal card layout left empty on black, with three bold question marks in the middle; the enemy hand shows a card back with a keyhole seal.

• Scorched Earth: stops the creature's triggered effects, entry ("When Ready") effects included. Its ability still works.
• Barbed Snare: only moving out counts as leaving; dying on the slot or being replaced doesn't.
• Watchdog conditions: each Watchdog card gets its own filter (ad hoc); shared templates may come later for filters that repeat. The number filter stays an attack's Attack for now.
• Abilities on cards: Day Trader (Insider Job), Pluriemployed Hustler (Nickel-and-Dime), Spite-Elected President (Spite), Supreme Court (Return to Sender), Hipster (Too Mainstream), Keyboard Warrior (No U); ten new test creatures carry the rest.
• Pictures: Its A Trap (Homer and the free-boat sting), Burn Notice (the Banksy self-shredding painting), Antivirus (a panda), Counter-Intelligence (the first Counter-Strike cover with accountants and calculators). Red Tape, YOLO and Preemptive Strike got briefs.

• Action Priority: removed; order inside a layer is Speed. Red Tape now adds Deployment Time, YOLO gives +4 Attack for this Day, Preemptive Strike is plain damage. Docs use Speed where they used Priority.
• Cleanse / Purge: Cleanse removes only what harms (bad statuses, stat decreases); Purge removes good and bad. Mixed statuses count as bad.
• Heresy: if the vessel is Doomed already, it fizzles and the creature stays Doomed.
• Moves: only the slot a creature lands on counts as entered. Planned moves show ghost cards where creatures will end up.
• Hidden effects in hot-seat: player 1 (the screen) sees only their own.
• Timed Slot Effects: "2 Days" is this Day and the next (used to stall).
• Abilities vs. effects: separate things; one ability, many triggered effects; what switches them off says which (Abilities Compendium).
• Identity Theft: the creature's action for the Day; hold Shift while dragging it onto the target.
• Underground: like Unaware, both ways.
• Watchdog Redirect: to a random creature; Heat Signature draws it first (most Heat).
• Watchdog charges: one per action it changes.
• Plot Armor: made, with a "once per combat" rule that lasts through reshuffles.
• Waiting for other systems: Watchdog Create, an AI opponent, the remaining Slot Effects (Revealed, Anchored, Banish...).
