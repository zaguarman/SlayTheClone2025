Open Questions

Questions that came up while implementing the GDD mechanics. Each says what was done in the meantime, so nothing waits on the answer.


ACTION PRIORITY (GDD 3.7)

1. Printed Priority on a creature card
    • Done for now: a creature's printed Priority applies to its attacks only ("Priority Attack +1"), as in the GDD example. Its abilities and its summon use 0 (plus Boosts and Penalties).
    • Question: should a creature card also be able to print Priority on its summon or abilities?

2. How long "for 1 Day" lasts on a Priority change
    • Done for now: a Priority Boost or Penalty isn't a status, so it works from the moment it's applied and its Days count the current one: "for 1 Day" ends at this Day's Dusk; "this Day and the next 3 Days" is 4 Days.
    • Question: is that right, or should Priority changes follow Status Timing (skip the Day they're applied)?

3. Side-wide Priority and spells
    • Done for now: "Actions by creatures you control get +1 Priority" only affects creatures' actions (moves aren't affected, they use the Automatic Move Order). Your spells keep their printed Priority.
    • Question: should there also be effects that boost your spells' Priority?

4. Cards waiting on a Priority design
    • Done: Preemptive Strike now resolves with Priority +1, as its design notes said.
    • Red Tape: its notes say "+1 Deployment Time on an enemy creature, or Priority -1 on its action". It still stuns. Should it become Priority -1 for 1 Day (instead of, or alongside, the stun)?
    • YOLO: its notes say "+1 Priority on its attack this Day, then Banished at Dusk". Banish doesn't exist yet. Add the +1 Priority now and leave the Banish for later?
    • Police Officer Productivity Target: "Priority attacks against creatures that moved this Day" needs a condition on the attack. Not done.
    • Temporal Distortion (flip Speed order for X Days) isn't made; no card uses it yet.


CLEANSE, BLESSED AND HERESY (Status Effects Compendium)

5. Does Cleanse remove positive statuses too?
    • Done for now: yes. A Cleanse removes every cleansable status, friendly or not, because Blessed (a positive status) is listed as "Cleansable: Yes (Removes all current stacks)". The GDD's Cleansing glossary entry says "negative status effects" though.
    • Question: should a Cleanse only remove negative statuses (and Blessed be removed only by an enemy "dispel")?

6. Does "Temporary Stat Modifications" include buffs?
    • Done for now: a Cleanse of stat changes removes every temporary one, buffs and debuffs alike. Base-stat (Persistent) changes stay. Priority Boosts and Penalties aren't stats, so they stay too.
    • Question: buffs and debuffs both, or only debuffs on your own creatures? Should Priority changes count as cleansable?

7. Heresy onto a creature that's already Doomed
    • Done for now: the vessel is the creature with the lowest current Health, even if it's Doomed already. A Doomed creature ignores new statuses, so the passed sentence is lost and the cleansed creature is freed anyway.
    • Question: should Heresy skip creatures that are already Doomed, so the sentence always lands somewhere?

8. New Myth test cards
    • The Holy Grail (Blessed 2 on a friendly creature) and Exorcism (Cleanse statuses on any creature) were added to test these. Names from the Brainstorm List (Myth > Legendary Objects & Places, Practices & Phenomena). They replaced Fat Tony, Martial Law and Hostile Takeover in the Mechanics Test deck, together with Preemptive Strike.
    • Question: are the stats and pictures right for them?


MOVEMENT LEFTOVERS (GDD 2.4)

9. Ghost cards at the final positions
    • Done for now: nothing new. The formation arrows already show where every creature ends up, including the ones pushed or swapped. A ghost card at each destination would overlap the ghosts of creatures queued to be summoned there.
    • Question: do you still want ghost cards for moves, or are the arrows enough?

10. AI move planning
    • There's no AI opponent in the game yet (both sides are played by hand), so there's nothing to teach about moves. It waits for the AI.


SLOT EFFECTS & ENVIRONMENT ACTIONS (GDD 2.4, 3.7, 'Slot Effects Compendium')

11. Which slots does a walking creature "enter"?
    • Done for now: only the slot a move ends on counts as entered (and the one it started from as left). Walking past an empty slot with a Landmine doesn't set it off; neither does leaping over one.
    • Question: should walking through a slot count as entering it?

12. Who sees hidden Slot Effects in hot-seat play?
    • Done for now: the screen is player 1's: player 1's hidden Slot Effects show (marked "hidden"), player 2's don't, until they trigger. With an AI opponent this becomes the real rule.
    • Question: fine for now?

13. Scorched Earth and entry effects
    • Done for now: Scorched Earth stops every triggered ability of the creature on it (retaliation, Dawn, Dusk, death effects) but not its entry ("When Ready") effects, which the GDD treats as part of the summon. Moving off the slot ends it.
    • Question: should entry effects also be stopped?

14. How long a timed Slot Effect lasts
    • Done for now: like Priority changes, its Days count the one it's placed: Ceasefire for 2 Days lasts this Day and the next, ending at that Dusk. Slot Effects aren't statuses, so Status Timing doesn't apply.
    • Question: right?

15. Barbed Snare and dying
    • Done for now: only leaving by moving (on its own, pushed or swapped) sets it off. A creature that dies or is replaced on the slot doesn't.
    • Question: should dying or being replaced also count as leaving?

16. Slot Effects implemented so far
    • Done: Landmine, Barbed Snare, Temporal Shield, Scorched Earth, Cleanse (SlotEffect), environment-first resolution.
    • Waiting on other mechanics: Surveillance Post (Revealed), Binding Chains and Threshold of Liminality (Anchored, Exposed), Rejection Field (return to hand), Rally Point (scheduled draws), Slippery Floor (needs the move's direction), Jamming Field (blocks spell targeting), Unstable Terrain, Echoing Cave, Sacrifice Lair (Banish), Magnetic Pulse (pull), Sanctuary (untargetable).
    • The four new cards (Lost Landmine, Ceasefire, Its A Trap, Burn Notice) have no art yet; their briefs are in their Art Description.


CREATURE ABILITIES ('Abilities Compendium')

17. Abilities vs. card effects
    • Done for now: the compendium's "one ability per creature" is a new slot on the creature card (printed in bold with reminder text), separate from its triggered card effects ("When this dies, ..."). Signal Jammer, Hive Mind, Copypasta and the rest only touch that slot. Exhausted (Abilities Disabled) and Deployment Time switch both off.
    • Question: is that the intent, or should triggered card effects count as the creature's ability too?

18. Which cards get abilities?
    • Done for now: every ability works and is tested, but no card has one yet, because the compendium says factions are assigned once cards are rebalanced.
    • Question: which factions and cards should get which abilities? Test cards for the Mechanics Test deck can follow.

19. Identity Theft isn't implemented
    • It's an Active ability ("swap abilities with target creature"), and there's no way yet to activate an ability (no active-ability action or UI).
    • Question: how should a player activate it: as the creature's intent for the Day (instead of attacking or moving), by dragging it onto a target?

20. Underground when it attacks
    • Done for now: when Underground is attacked, the attacker hits with its base Attack (printed + Persistent changes; its buffs are ignored). When Underground attacks, the defender's stat changes would be its Health buffs, and ignoring a Health buff mid-combat isn't defined, so nothing changes yet.
    • Question: what should "ignore the defender's stat changes" do when Underground attacks: deal extra damage equal to its temporary Health bonus?

21. Two Signal Jammers facing each other
    • Done for now: a jammer's own on/off state is judged without other jammers, so two facing jammers switch each other off.

22. What counts as "an effect on more than one creature" (Too Mainstream)
    • Done for now: effects on every creature of a group ("each enemy creature"), effects that also hit adjacent creatures, attack spreads and chains. An effect that picks one creature at random, or is aimed at it, still affects it.



WATCHDOGS (GDD 3.11)

23. What's done and what waits
    • Done: Watchdogs created through the queue by a card effect, watching a creature (when the effect is aimed at one) or a whole side. Conditions: action type (Attack, Spell, Move, Ability, Summon, Status), side, creature, and "N or less" (an attack's Attack). Effects: Nullify, Redirect, Reorder, Reveal. On Watch and Briefing timings, charges, Days, the oldest-match rule, and catching actions queued before the Watchdog existed.
    • Waiting: "Can't match the unseen" (Concealed, Undercover, Encrypted don't exist yet); Reveal does nothing yet, because everything is visible (no Fog of War); Create (queue a new action) needs a way to describe that action on a card; the Turn History entry ("nullified by ...") is only in the log for now.

24. Redirect: to where?
    • Done for now: to a random other creature on the side the action was aimed at (for attacks and aimed spells). If there's none, the action is unchanged. Moves and summons can't be redirected.
    • Question: should the card say where it redirects (e.g., "to the enemy's weakest creature", "back at its own side")?

25. "Every" at the Briefing
    • Done for now: picking every matching action costs one charge in total, not one per action.
    • Question: right, or one charge per action?

26. "N or less" for non-attacks
    • Done for now: the number only applies to attacks (their Attack). Spells, statuses and abilities have no strength to compare.
    • Question: what should it measure for those (damage dealt, Days of a status)?

27. Watchdog visibility in hot-seat
    • Done for now: each side lists its Watchdogs at the end of its row. Player 1's show in full; player 2's show only what they watch, with the condition as "???".

28. New test cards
    • Antivirus (Tech, Brainstorm List > Tech > Core Technologies): Watchdog: enemy spells -> Nullify, 1 charge (On Watch).
    • Counter-Intelligence (Shadow Ops., Brainstorm List > Shadow Ops. > Espionage): Watchdog: the strongest enemy attack -> Redirect, 2 charges (Briefing).
    • They replaced Digital Nomad and Raised Security Level in the Mechanics Test deck (Delivery Cyclist and Diplomat still test Leapfrog and Armor).
    • Question: are the stats and pictures right?
