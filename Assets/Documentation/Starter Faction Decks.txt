1. PURPOSE

• Three small monofaction decks (16–17 cards) that play today with the rules the engine already has: damage, healing, armor, draws, summoning from the deck, stat changes (ModifyStat), and the Paralyzed stun.
• No faction statuses, Deployment Time tricks, Priority, Interference, Watchdogs, control changes or Locations are used.
• Every card is a placeholder for a future GDD version. That version is stored on the card asset itself (the "Design Notes" field in the Inspector) and summarized below as "Future".
• Source of truth: Assets/Scripts/Generators/FactionDecksGenerator.cs (menu: Cards > Generate Faction Decks). Change cards there and run it again: cards keep their ids and art.
• Art: Tools/generate_card_art.py, then Cards > Assign Card Art. Corporate uses an office skyline with a stock line, Statecraft a propaganda poster, Zeitgeist a vaporwave sunset.
• Decks: Assets/Scriptables/Decks/CorporateDeck, StatecraftDeck, ZeitgeistDeck. Assign one to Player 1 or Player 2 Deck on GameReferences to play it.


2. HOW THE PLACEHOLDERS MAP TO THE GDD

Faction costs (Faction Compendium, "Pays With")
    • Corporate (Capital, discard cards): Discarding as a cost doesn't exist yet. Its strongest spells give the enemy a card instead ("the enemy draws a card").
    • Statecraft (Bureaucracy): Implemented as is. Its creatures take 1 turn longer to deploy than similar ones, and Curfew stuns your own side.
    • Zeitgeist (Self-harm): Implemented as is. Damage to its own creatures and to you.

Faction holes (Faction Compendium, "Can't")
    • Corporate never heals. Statecraft never hides anything (nothing hides yet). Zeitgeist has no Intelligence (nothing reveals yet).

Mechanic stand-ins
    • Red Tape (+Deployment Time) -> Stun 1 turn.
    • Priority strikes -> high Speed.
    • Recruitment -> Summon random creatures from your own deck.
    • Investments / Persistent Stat Modifications -> ModifyStat, which lasts while the creature stays on the battlefield.
    • Worker tokens -> Unpaid Intern copies in the deck.
    • Burnout / Coffeinated -> "At the end of each turn, deal 1 damage to this."
    • Strikes -> Stun each creature on both sides.
    • OnDeploymentTimeComplete -> "When this enters" on a creature with Deployment Time, which already fires when it becomes operative.


3. CORPORATE: SHAREHOLDER VALUE (17 cards)

Play style: cheap workers that die usefully, card draw, and stat growth on whoever is lucky. Wins by out-drawing and out-growing.

Creatures (Attack/Health/Speed, Deployment Time)
    • Unpaid Intern x3 (1/1/3, 0): When this dies, draw a card.
        • Future: a Worker token created by other cards; sacrifice fodder.
    • Gig Courier x2 (3/1/3, 0): No ability.
        • Future: leaves play on its own after 3 Days.
    • Headhunter (1/2/2, 0): When this enters, summon a random creature from your deck.
        • Future: summons an Unpaid Intern token.
    • Middle Manager (2/3/1, 1): Each turn, a random friendly creature gets +1 Attack.
        • Future: Coffeinated on a Worker.
    • Venture Capitalist (1/4/1, 1): Each turn, a random friendly creature gets +1/+1.
        • Future: an Investment that pays a Persistent buff on a later Day.
    • Unicorn Startup (1/8/1, 2): Draws a card each turn, takes 1 damage each Dusk.
        • Future: Unicorn (Enterprise).
    • Golden Parachute CEO (3/4/1, 2): When this dies, draw 2 cards.
        • Future: also buffs a random friendly creature (failing upward).

Spells
    • Layoffs: 2 damage to each friendly creature, draw 3.
        • Future: sacrifice Workers as a cost, 1 draw per Worker.
    • Team-Building Retreat: Each friendly creature gets +1 Attack.
        • Future: Coffeinated, then Burnout.
    • Trickle-Down Economics: A random friendly creature gets +3/+3.
        • Future: always targets your strongest creature.
    • Performance Review: Target creature gets -2 Attack.
        • Future: Persistent.
    • Paywall: 1 damage to each enemy creature.
        • Future: Paywall tax on the enemy's Deployment Time 0 plays.
    • Quarterly Earnings Call: Draw 2; the enemy draws 1.
        • Future: discard a card as the cost instead.
    • Hostile Takeover: 4 damage to any target; the enemy draws 1.
        • Future: discard 2 cards as the cost instead.

6. NEXT STEPS

• Card text has no Faction display yet beyond the type line ("Creature — Corporate"); a faction frame color or icon would help.
• Balance is untested. Start by playing each deck against the others.
• Known bug #45 (two summons from the deck in one turn can land in the same slot) affects Conscription and Headhunter.
• When a GDD mechanic lands, update the matching cards in FactionDecksGenerator.cs and clear their Design Notes.
