Scorched Earth
    Visibility: Visible

Slippery Floor
    Visibility: Hidden
    Trigger Event(s): OnEnterSlot(movingCreature, enteredSlot, method) (Slot effect logic checks: if enteredSlot is this slot).
    Effect: The movingCreature slides one extra slot in the direction it was moving, if that slot is empty and it can be moved. If it can't (the board's edge, an occupied slot, Heavy or multi-slot, or it entered without a direction, e.g., summoned), it takes 2 damage instead. The Slippery Floor is then revealed and consumed.
    Notes: Being slid isn't the creature's own movement. The extra slot it lands on counts as entered (OnEnterSlot).
    Parameters: Damage if not pushed: 2 (set by applying card).
    Duration of Slot Effect: 1 Trigger (consumed immediately after its effect is queued, as per GDD 3.11 Instant Effects).
    Cleansable: Yes.
