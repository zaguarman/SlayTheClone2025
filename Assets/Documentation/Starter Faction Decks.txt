1. PURPOSE

• Three monofaction decks, one per faction, 16 different cards each (10 creatures and 6 spells). No copies yet.
• Every card name comes from the 'Brainstorm List' or the 'Refined Brainstorm List', picking the most cynical ones. Margin Call is the exception: a Corporate staple added on purpose.
• Cards only use what the engine already does: damage, healing, armor, draws, summoning from the deck, stat changes (ModifyStat), and the Paralyzed stun.
• No faction statuses, Deployment Time tricks, Priority, Interference, Watchdogs, control changes or Locations.
• No card checks or rewards the presence of another faction (no "deals more damage to Corporate creatures").
• Every card is a placeholder for a future GDD version. That version is stored on the card asset itself (the "Design Notes" field in the Inspector) and summarized below as "Future".

Where things live
    • Cards: Assets/Scripts/Generators/FactionDecksGenerator.cs (menu: Cards > Generate Faction Decks). Running it again updates the cards in place, keeps their ids and art, and deletes card assets no longer in the list.
    • Art: Tools/generate_card_art.py, then Cards > Assign Card Art. Corporate uses an office skyline with a stock line, Statecraft a propaganda poster, Zeitgeist a vaporwave sunset.
    • Decks: Assets/Scriptables/Decks/CorporateDeck, StatecraftDeck, ZeitgeistDeck.
    • Main menu: Assets/Scriptables/Decks/DeckCatalog lists the decks you can pick and one opponent per deck. Play opens the match setup: your deck, your opponent, and their deck (they bring their own unless you swap it).
    • Opening the game scene directly plays Corporate against Statecraft (the decks set on GameReferences).


2. HOW THE PLACEHOLDERS MAP TO THE GDD

Faction costs (Faction Compendium, "Pays With")
    • Corporate (Capital, discard cards): Discarding as a cost doesn't exist yet. Some cards give the enemy a card or a buff instead (Day Trader, Hostile Takeover, Multimillionaire Philanthropist), or hurt you later (Charles Ponzi).
    • Statecraft (Bureaucracy): Implemented as is. Its creatures take 1–2 turns to deploy, and Martial Law stuns your own side.
    • Zeitgeist (Self-harm): Implemented as is. Damage to its own creatures and to you, and chaos that hits both sides.

Faction holes (Faction Compendium, "Can't")
    • Corporate never heals. Statecraft never hides anything (nothing hides yet). Zeitgeist has no Intelligence (nothing reveals yet).

Mechanic stand-ins
    • Red Tape (+Deployment Time) -> Stun 1 turn.
    • Priority strikes -> high Speed.
    • Recruitment -> summon random creatures from your own deck.
    • Investments / Persistent Stat Modifications -> ModifyStat, which lasts while the creature stays on the battlefield.
    • Burnout / Coffeinated -> "At the end of each turn, deal 1 damage to this."
    • Strikes -> stun every creature on both sides.
    • OnDeploymentTimeComplete -> "When this enters" on a creature with Deployment Time, which already fires when it becomes operative.


3. CORPORATE: SHAREHOLDER VALUE (10 creatures, 6 spells)

Play style: an engine that draws cards off everything, spends its own people as fuel and lets someone else pay the bill.

Creatures (Attack/Health/Speed, Deployment Time)
    • Unpaid Intern (1/1/3, 0): When this dies, draw a card.
        • Future: a Worker token created by other cards; sacrifice fodder.
    • Inhuman Resources (1/4/1, 1): Each turn, 1 damage to a random friendly creature and draw a card.
        • Future: Layoffs as an additional cost.
    • Micromanager (2/3/1, 1): Each turn, a random friendly creature gets +1 Attack.
        • Future: Coffeinated on a Worker.
    • Multilevel Marketing Seller (1/2/2, 0): When this enters, summon a random creature from your deck.
        • Future: summons an Unpaid Intern token.
    • Day Trader (3/1/3, 0): When this enters, draw a card. When this dies, the enemy draws a card.
        • Future: an Investment that pays out or loses cards.
    • Pluriemployed Hustler (2/3/2, 0): When this enters, draw 2. Takes 1 damage each Dusk.
        • Future: Coffeinated, then Burnout.
    • Unicorn (1/8/1, 2): Draws a card each turn, takes 1 damage each Dusk.
        • Future: Unicorn (Enterprise).
    • Multimillionaire Philanthropist (2/5/1, 2): When this enters, each enemy creature gets +1 Health and you draw 2.
        • Future: a Contract; your tax break arrives on a later Day.
    • Fat Tony (4/5/1, 2): When this enters, 2 damage to a random enemy creature.
        • Future: An Offer You Can't Refuse (Recruitment).
    • Charles Ponzi (1/3/2, 1): Draws a card each turn. When this dies, 3 damage to you.
        • Future: pays draws until it collapses, then takes them back.

Spells
    • Margin Call: 2 damage to each creature (both sides), draw 2.
        • Future: discard your hand as the cost; damage scales with it.
    • Hostile Takeover: 4 damage to any target; the enemy draws 1.
        • Future: discard 2 cards as the cost instead.
    • Mass Layoffs: 2 damage to each friendly creature, draw 3.
        • Future: sacrifice Workers as a cost, 1 draw per Worker.
    • Team Building Exercise: Each friendly creature gets +1 Attack.
        • Future: Coffeinated, then Burnout.
    • Dead Cat Bounce: Target creature gets +3 Attack and -2 Health.
        • Future: a Temporary buff followed by a Persistent loss.
    • Taxpayer Funds Venture Capital Profits: Draw 2; 2 damage to the enemy player.
        • Future: the enemy draws 1 fewer card instead.

6. NEXT STEPS

• Balance is untested. Start by playing each deck against the others from the main menu.
• There is no computer opponent yet: both sides are played by you on the same screen.
• Known bug #45 (two summons from the deck in one turn can land in the same slot) can affect Multilevel Marketing Seller.
• When a GDD mechanic lands, update the matching cards in FactionDecksGenerator.cs and clear their Design Notes.
• Names left out on purpose: brainstorm entries that mock groups of people rather than institutions or behaviours.
