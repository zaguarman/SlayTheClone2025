ADVENTURE SIMULATION

30 runs a faction, 1868 fights in all. Each run: the faction's starter deck, 60 Health, rooms picked at random along
the paths. You are played by the Hard AI; the enemies as the rooms set them (Easy early, Medium, Hard elites,
Very Hard boss; Health 5, 10, 15... and 60 for the boss). Won, a run takes the strongest card offered (its own faction's);
shops buy the strongest card it can pay, then take out the weakest. Events and rest sites are walked through.
A fight still going after 40 Days counts as lost (stalled).
