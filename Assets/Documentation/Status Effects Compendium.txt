**DOCUMENT USAGE GUIDELINES:**

*   **Comprehensive List:** This document contains the definitive list of all standardized status effects in the game.
*   **Stability of Definitions:** The names, core effects, durations, and key properties (e.g., removability, stacking) of these status effects are stable. Do not alter without explicit design approval.
*   **Adding New Status Effects:** New status effects should only be added if a fundamentally new type of condition is required that cannot be adequately represented by existing status effects or their interactions.
*   **GDD Context:** Refer to the main Game Design Document (GDD) for how these status effects interact with broader game systems, abilities, and trigger events. This compendium provides definitions; the GDD provides application and interaction context.
*   **Keyword Glossary:** Refer to the 'Keyword Glossary' for definitions of terms used within these status effect descriptions (e.g., `Concealed Action`).

---

*   **Addicted:** If stats didn't change on a turn it will turn into `Exhausted`. Speed +1. Not removable. *(Triggered by `Coffeinated`)*
*   **Anchored:** Can't move by own created move actions, but can be moved by other creatures or spells. Remove on moved move successfuly.
*   **Blessed:** Persists until consumed. Negates the next status effect that would be applied, then `Blessed` is removed. Can stack up to 3 times. Theme: Single-use ward, divine protection.
*   **Bloodthirst:** Has to attack every turn, if this turn didn't attack halve current hp at the end of the turn, round down (e.g., 10/10 -> 5/10 -> 2/10 -> 1/10 -> 0/10). When doing damage (any kind) receive half of damage done (rounded up) as health.
*   **Bored:** Has to move/be moved or receives X damage per turn.
*   **Brainwashed:** After 2 turns, at the end of the turn the creature will change owner (to the player applying effect), all abilities are disabled. Status effects are kept.
*   **Burnout:** Creature gets a burnout stack on itself. Doesn't reduce over time. When the burnout reaches 3 stacks this and all other creatures with burnout stacks receive 3 damage. If tried to remove explodes immediately and friendly nearby creatures gain 1 stack of burnout. The flavouring can be as a cost for using powerful abilities or as a magical curse.
*   **Chill:** Halves attack, restore health per turn. X turns.
*   **Coffeinated:** Raises attack and speed but consumes 1 health every turn, triggers `Addicted` after 2 turns.
*   **Cognitive Dissonance:** Immune to all positive stat changes, reduce damage taken from all sources by 1. Can't be removed.
*   **Compromised (Persists until cleansed or creature leaves play):** Flavor: An agent captured and converted into a double agent.
    *   **Targeting:** Typically applied to an opponent's creature that is currently "Undercover" or has the `Concealed` status effect.
    *   **Effect on Visibility (for the Handler):** For the player who applied `Compromised` (the "handler"):
        *   All past '???' entries in the Turn History from this creature become fully visible to the handler.
        *   All future actions queued by this creature, even if they would normally be `Concealed Actions` (due to "Undercover" ability or ongoing `Concealed` status), are fully visible to the handler in their Turn History after resolution.
    *   **Effect on Visibility (for others):** The original controller of the `Compromised` creature, and any other opponents, still see its `Concealed Actions` as '???' in the Turn History.
    *   **"On Being Revealed" Triggers:** Passive information leakage to the handler due to `Compromised` status does *not* automatically trigger any `OnRevealedByIntelligence` effects.
    *   **Optional Intelligence Action:** The handler can *still choose* to use a separate Intelligence action targeting a past concealed action. If they do so, associated `OnRevealedByIntelligence` Traps or Strategic Disclosures *will* trigger.
    *   Theme: Double agent, leaky information.
*   **Concealed (X turns):** For X turns, all actions queued by this creature are `Concealed Actions`.
    *   `Concealed Actions` are not visible to the opponent pre-resolution and appear as '???' in their Turn History.
    *   Negated by `Revealed` status.
    *   Theme: Secrets, hidden operations.
*   **Deployment Time (X turns):** Applied on entry. For X turns, this creature cannot perform actions (Attacks, Active Abilities) and its passive abilities do not trigger (exceptions possible). Decreases by 1 at start of controller's turn. Duration can be reduced by effects. Not removable by general `Cleanse` actions. Theme: Unit powering up.
*   **Diplomatic Immunity:** Immune to spells (friendly/unfriendly). Abilities, combat damage, and status effects still apply.
*   **Doomed (X turns):** Upon application, remove all other status effects from this creature. While `Doomed`, this creature is immune to gaining any other status effects. Destroyed after X turns. Gains +1 Attack at the start of each turn. `Doomed` itself and its associated effects cannot be disabled. Cannot be removed by `Cleanse` effects. Doesn't stack or renew duration. Theme: Marked for death, final surge.
*   **Earworm:** Take 1 damage per turn. 2 turn duration. If not removed after 2 turns spreads to nearby friendly creatures.
*   **Exhausted:** Completely disabled (stun-like effect, everything disabled including abilities and triggers). Gain also `Exposed`. Not removable. *(Can be triggered by `Addicted`)*
*   **Exposed:** Take additional damage from all sources (+1 damage). Applies `Revealed` too.
*   **Ghosted:** Doesn't block friendly movement. Loses 1 health every turn. Can't trigger slot effects. Can't be targeted by direct attacks. After 3 turns remove it and apply `Doomed (X turns)`.
*   **Heat Signature:** All homing attacks (friendly and unfriendly) hit this target while status effect is active.
*   **Heavy:** For X turns, the creature cannot perform Move actions and cannot be moved by other effects. Can only attack the opposite slot (this overrides all targeting). Its attack is doubled.
*   **Hipster:** Ignore stat changes in enemy creatures (good or bad) after 2 turns will become `Ostracised`. Remove `Hipster` when that happens.
*   **Honeypot:** If this creature or created actions are targeted by intelligence, retaliate.
*   **Impatient:** Has to attack every turn or receives X damage per turn.
*   **Imposter Syndrome:** Cannot have base stats increased from friendly effects, if it happens player gets 1 damage.
*   **Invisible status effect:** (Conceptual placeholder) A status that shows subtle signs and evolves into a worse status if not addressed.
*   **Keep Calm:** Creature priority changes are disabled (it does everything on the normal priority, negative priority is also ignored).
*   **Muted:** Creature abilities dont trigger during X turns.
*   **Ostracised:** Cannot be targeted by spells/actions/abilities (friendly/unfriendly), neither can attack or be attacked, abilities are disabled, status effects still apply. Lose 1 health every turn end. This status effect fades after 2 Turns. *(Can be triggered by `Hipster`)*
*   **Pandora:** Every turn at the start randomly adds 1 minor removable status effect to a friendly creature including itself (it can be good or bad). 3 Turn duration. Can't be removed.
*   **Paranoid:** Next turn will attack friendly creature at random if not removed.
*   **Resurrected:** Damaged by healing, speed decreased by 1 (min 1). Can't be removed.
*   **Revealed (X turns):** For X turns, actively counters concealment.
    *   If a creature with an "Undercover" ability or `Concealed` status gains `Revealed`, its actions are *not* `Concealed Actions`.
    *   Applying `Revealed` can trigger `OnRevealedByIntelligence` effects if it causes a creature to stop producing `Concealed Actions`.
    *   Overrides `Concealed` status and "Undercover" ability's concealment.
    *   Theme: Exposed, under surveillance.
*   **Slacking Off:** Priority lowered by 2, heal 2 points per turn.
*   **Take Cover:** Halves Speed, gains armor per turn. X turns. Can't attack.
