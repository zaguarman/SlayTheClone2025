Gameplay:

Creatures evolve over time in the board, the deployment time tells how much turns do you need to go to the next step
On every evolution the creature unlocks more abilities
this abilities can have side effects like, get status effect X or gain +5/+5/+0 

Example would be Snail; 0/1/1; Deployment time 2 (2 turns for each step); Step 1: Creature gains Spikes (Return x damage to attacker); Step 2: creature cleanses status effects; creature gains status effect Doomed.
