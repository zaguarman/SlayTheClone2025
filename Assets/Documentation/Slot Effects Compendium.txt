**DOCUMENT USAGE GUIDELINES (for this Slot Effects Compendium):**

*   **Definitive List:** This document contains the definitive list and specific definitions of all standardized Slot Effects in the game.
*   **Core Mechanics in GDD:** For general rules on how Slot Effects function (e.g., exclusivity, replacement, visibility, cleansing, controller, consumption of "1 Trigger" effects), refer to the main Game Design Document (GDD), specifically Section III.B.3 and Section II.H (Meta-Actions). The controller of a Slot Effect is the player who applied it.
*   **Individual Effect Details:** Each entry below specifies the individual Slot Effect's name, its default visibility, its specific trigger event(s) from the 'Trigger Events List', its game effect, any parameters, its duration, and whether it is cleansable.
*   **Turn Structure:** All "Turns" referenced in this document are global Turns, experienced simultaneously by all players, as defined in the GDD.

---

**Surveillance Post**
    *   **Visibility:** Hidden
    *   **Trigger Event(s):** `OnEnterSlot(movingCreature, enteredSlot, method)` (Slot effect logic checks: if `enteredSlot` is this slot and `movingCreature` is an enemy to the controller of this Slot Effect).
    *   **Effect:** The `movingCreature` gains `Revealed (2 Turns)`. The Surveillance Post is then consumed.
    *   **Parameters:** None.
    *   **Duration of Slot Effect:** 1 Trigger (consumed immediately after its effect is queued, as per GDD II.H.4).
    *   **Cleansable:** Yes

**Temporal Shield**
    *   **Visibility:** Visible
    *   **Trigger Event(s):** Continuous while active.
    *   **Effect:** Any creature on this slot is immune to combat damage.
    *   **Parameters:** Duration: X (e.g., 1).
    *   **Duration of Slot Effect:** X global Turns, then removed.
    *   **Cleansable:** Yes
    *   **Notes:** Does not prevent other effects like status applications or damage from Spells/abilities unless the damage itself was the carrier and specified as combat damage.

**Landmine**
    *   **Visibility:** Hidden
    *   **Trigger Event(s):** `OnEnterSlot(movingCreature, enteredSlot, method)` (Slot effect logic checks: if `enteredSlot` is this slot).
    *   **Effect:** The `movingCreature` takes X damage. The Landmine is then consumed.
    *   **Parameters:** Damage: X (e.g., 2).
    *   **Duration of Slot Effect:** 1 Trigger (consumed immediately after its effect is queued, as per GDD II.H.4).
    *   **Cleansable:** Yes

**Binding Chains**
    *   **Visibility:** Visible
    *   **Trigger Event(s):** `OnBeforeLeaveSlot(movingCreature, originSlot, intendedDestinationSlot)` (Slot effect logic checks: if `originSlot` is this slot).
    *   **Effect:** The `movingCreature` gains `Anchored`. The Binding Chains is then consumed.
    *   **Parameters:** None.
    *   **Duration of Slot Effect:** 1 Trigger (consumed immediately after its effect is queued, as per GDD II.H.4).
    *   **Cleansable:** Yes

**Rejection Field**
    *   **Visibility:** Visible
    *   **Trigger Event(s):** `OnEnterSlot(movingCreature, enteredSlot, method)` (Slot effect logic checks: if `enteredSlot` is this slot).
    *   **Effect:** The `movingCreature` entering this slot is returned to its owner's hand.
    *   **Parameters:** None.
    *   **Duration of Slot Effect:** Persistent until replaced.
    *   **Cleansable:** Yes

**Scorched Earth**
    *   **Visibility:** Visible
    *   **Trigger Event(s):** Continuous while active and slot is occupied.
    *   **Effect:** Passive abilities of any creature occupying this slot do not trigger.
    *   **Parameters:** None.
    *   **Duration of Slot Effect:** Persistent until replaced.
    *   **Cleansable:** Yes

**Rally Point**
    *   **Visibility:** Visible
    *   **Trigger Event(s):** `OnTurnStart` (Slot effect logic checks if its slot is occupied by a creature controlled by the same player who controls this Rally Point).
    *   **Effect:** If a creature controlled by this slot effect's controller is on this slot at the Start of a Turn, its controller Schedules 1 additional card draw for their next Turn.
    *   **Parameters:** None.
    *   **Duration of Slot Effect:** Persistent until replaced.
    *   **Cleansable:** Yes

**Slippery Floor**
    *   **Visibility:** Hidden
    *   **Trigger Event(s):** `OnEnterSlot(movingCreature, enteredSlot, method)` (Slot effect logic checks: if `enteredSlot` is this slot).
    *   **Effect:** The `movingCreature` is immediately pushed one slot directly backward (towards its controller's edge of the board) if the destination slot is empty and valid. If it cannot be pushed (e.g., at edge of board, next slot occupied), it instead takes 2 damage. The Slippery Floor is then consumed.
    *   **Parameters:** Damage if not pushed: 2.
    *   **Duration of Slot Effect:** 1 Trigger (consumed immediately after its effect is queued, as per GDD II.H.4).
    *   **Cleansable:** Yes.

**Jamming Field**
    *   **Visibility:** Visible
    *   **Trigger Event(s):** Continuous while active.
    *   **Effect:** Spells cannot be played targeting this slot or any creature occupying it. Additionally, active and passive abilities of any creature occupying this slot are `Abilities Disabled`.
    *   **Parameters:** None.
    *   **Duration of Slot Effect:** Persistent until replaced.
    *   **Cleansable:** Yes

**Unstable Terrain**
    *   **Visibility:** Visible
    *   **Trigger Event(s):** `OnTurnEnd` (Slot effect logic checks if its slot is occupied).
    *   **Effect:** If this is the 2nd consecutive Turn the *same* creature ends its Turn on this slot, that creature is moved to a random available adjacent friendly slot (if multiple, chosen randomly). If no such slot is available, nothing happens. The "consecutive turn" counter resets if a different creature occupies the slot, the slot becomes empty, or the creature is successfully moved by this effect or any other means.
    *   **Parameters:** None.
    *   **Duration of Slot Effect:** Persistent until replaced.
    *   **Cleansable:** Yes

**Echoing Cave**
    *   **Visibility:** Visible
    *   **Trigger Event(s):** `OnActionQueued(actionDetails)` (Slot effect logic checks: if `actionDetails.sourceCreature` is on this slot and if the action is a 'PassiveAbilityEffect').
    *   **Effect:** If a creature on this slot triggers a passive ability, that passive ability's resulting action is duplicated in the Action Queue with the same targets and `Priority (Action)`. This effect triggers once, then Echoing Cave is removed.
    *   **Parameters:** None.
    *   **Duration of Slot Effect:** 1 Trigger (consumed immediately after its effect is queued, as per GDD II.H.4).
    *   **Cleansable:** Yes

**Sacrifice Lair**
    *   **Visibility:** Visible
    *   **Trigger Event(s):** `OnEnterSlot(movingCreature, enteredSlot, method)` (Slot effect logic checks: if `enteredSlot` is this slot).
    *   **Effect:** The `movingCreature` is Banished. The Sacrifice Lair is then consumed.
    *   **Parameters:** None
    *   **Duration of Slot Effect:** 1 Trigger (consumed immediately after its effect is queued, as per GDD II.H.4).
    *   **Cleansable:** Yes

**Barbed Snare**
    *   **Visibility:** Hidden
    *   **Trigger Event(s):** `OnBeforeLeaveSlot(movingCreature, originSlot, intendedDestinationSlot)` (Slot effect logic checks: if `originSlot` is this slot).
    *   **Effect:** The `movingCreature` takes X damage. The movement is still allowed (unless prevented by another effect). The Barbed Snare is then consumed.
    *   **Parameters:** Damage: X (e.g., 1).
    *   **Duration of Slot Effect:** 1 Trigger (consumed immediately after its effect is queued, as per GDD II.H.4).
    *   **Cleansable:** Yes

**Magnetic Pulse**
    *   **Visibility:** Visible
    *   **Trigger Event(s):** `OnTurnStart` (Slot effect logic checks if its slot is occupied by a creature controlled by the player who applied Magnetic Pulse).
    *   **Effect:** At the Start of the Turn, if their creature is on this slot, pull one random adjacent enemy creature one slot towards this Magnetic Pulse slot, if the intervening slot is empty and the target creature can be moved.
    *   **Parameters:** None.
    *   **Duration of Slot Effect:** Persistent until replaced.
    *   **Cleansable:** Yes

**Sanctuary**
    *   **Visibility:** Visible
    *   **Trigger Event(s):** Continuous while active.
    *   **Effect:** Creatures on this slot cannot be targeted by enemy Spells or enemy creature abilities.
    *   **Parameters:** None
    *   **Duration of Slot Effect:** Persistent until replaced.
    *   **Cleansable:** Yes

**Threshold of Liminality**
    *   **Visibility:** Visible
    *   **Trigger Event(s):** `OnEnterSlot(movingCreature, enteredSlot, method)` (Slot effect logic checks: if `enteredSlot` is this slot).
    *   **Effect:** When a creature enters this slot:
        *   The `movingCreature` gains `Anchored`.
        *   The `movingCreature` gains `Exposed (1 Turn)`.
        *   The controller of the `movingCreature` schedules 1 fewer card draw for their next Turn.
    *   **Parameters:** Duration: X (e.g., 2, as set by the applying card).
    *   **Duration of Slot Effect:** X global Turns, then removed.
    *   **Cleansable:** Yes
    *   **Notes:** The `Anchored` status applied by this effect persists as per its own rules (i.e., until the creature is successfully moved by another effect, or the status is cleansed), even if 'Threshold of Liminality' expires or is cleansed. `Exposed` status includes applying `Revealed` to the creature for the same duration.
