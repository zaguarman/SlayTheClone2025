**DOCUMENT USAGE GUIDELINES (for this Illustrative Card Examples List):**

*   **Illustrative Purpose:** This document provides examples of cards to demonstrate how game mechanics, keywords, status effects, and faction themes can be realized. These are not exhaustive or final card lists but serve as design aids and points of reference.
*   **Adherence to GDD:** All card examples **must** adhere to the principles, keywords, status effect definitions (and their precise notation), faction list, and mechanics (including Meta-Actions, action resolution, targeting, etc.) outlined in the main Game Design Document (GDD) and its associated compendia (Status Effects, Slot Effects, Trigger Events).
*   **Faction Alignment:** Each card must belong to one of the definitive factions listed in GDD Section II.A (Shadow Ops., Statecraft, Myth, Corporate, Digital Society, Zeitgeist, Mind, Tech, Utopia). Cards are strictly single-faction.
*   **Clarity over Brevity (in Examples):** Card text in these examples may sometimes include parenthetical notes (e.g., "(Mechanics: ...)") to clarify the underlying GDD rule being demonstrated. Final in-game card text would aim for conciseness while maintaining mechanical clarity based on established player knowledge of GDD rules.
*   **Placeholder Mechanics:** Any card concepts with "TODO" or placeholder mechanics must be resolved by mapping them to existing GDD mechanics or by proposing new mechanics for design approval and integration into the GDD before the card is considered viable.
*   **Flavor Text:** Flavor text is illustrative and aims to match the game's tone.
*   **Stability:** While specific card stats and effects here are illustrative, the *way* they use defined game elements (keywords, status effects, triggers) should be consistent with the GDD.
