Open Questions

Questions that came up while implementing the GDD mechanics. Each says what was done in the meantime, so nothing waits on the answer.


STILL OPEN

1. Scorched Earth (Slot Effect, visible, until replaced)
    • What it does now: the creature standing on that slot can't trigger its triggered effects (retaliation, Dawn and Dusk effects, death effects). Its entry ("When Ready") effects still happen. Moving off the slot ends it.
    • Question: with abilities and effects now separate, should it switch off the triggered effects (as now), the ability, or both? And should entry effects be stopped too?

2. Barbed Snare (Slot Effect, hidden, 1 trigger)
    • What it does now: the first creature to leave that slot, on its own, pushed or swapped, takes X damage; then the snare is used up and revealed.
    • Question: should a creature dying on the slot, or being replaced by a summon, also count as leaving?

3. Watchdog "N or less" for actions that aren't attacks
    • A Watchdog condition can include a number: "attacks of 3 or less" only reacts to attacks by creatures with 3 Attack or less. For attacks the number is clearly the attacker's Attack.
    • Question: what should the number compare when the Watchdog watches something else? Proposal: spells, their damage; statuses, their Days or stacks; moves, how many slots; summons, the creature's Attack. Or: the number only exists for attacks.

4. Which statuses are positive (only removed by a Purge)?
    • Answered in part: mixed ones count as bad. Only Blessed is marked positive so far; the rest is to be decided status by status.

5. Ability assignments (proposal, nothing applied yet)
    • Corporate: Insider Job (insider trading), Technician. E.g., Pluriemployed Hustler gets Technician.
    • Statecraft: Spite (Spite-Elected President), Return to Sender (Supreme Court).
    • Shadow Ops.: Underground (the Mole), Signal Jammer, Identity Theft.
    • Tech: Zero-Day, Unpatchable.

    • Myth: Hive Mind (it's the Mummy ability: a curse that spreads to whoever touches it).

    • Zeitgeist: Too Mainstream (Hipster).
    • Question: OK? Then the cards get them, and test cards follow.

6. Move ghosts show a "Queued" badge
    • The ghost of a creature in the slot it will move to reuses the queued-card look, badge included.
    • Question: keep it, or a different badge ("Moving") or none?

7. New cards: stats and pictures (listed in 'Starter Faction Decks', section 6)
    • Question: OK as they are?


ANSWERED (2026-10-12)

• Action Priority: removed; order inside a layer is Speed. Red Tape now adds Deployment Time, YOLO gives +4 Attack for this Day, Preemptive Strike is plain damage. Docs use Speed where they used Priority.
• Cleanse / Purge: Cleanse removes only what harms (bad statuses, stat decreases); Purge removes good and bad. Mixed statuses count as bad.
• Heresy: if the vessel is Doomed already, it fizzles and the creature stays Doomed.
• Moves: only the slot a creature lands on counts as entered. Planned moves show ghost cards where creatures will end up.
• Hidden effects in hot-seat: player 1 (the screen) sees only their own.
• Timed Slot Effects: "2 Days" is this Day and the next (used to stall).
• Abilities vs. effects: separate things; one ability, many triggered effects; what switches them off says which (Abilities Compendium).
• Identity Theft: the creature's action for the Day; hold Shift while dragging it onto the target.
• Underground: like Unaware, both ways.
• Watchdog Redirect: to a random creature; Heat Signature draws it first (most Heat).
• Watchdog charges: one per action it changes.
• Plot Armor: made, with a "once per combat" rule that lasts through reshuffles.
• Waiting for other systems: Fog of War (Concealed, Encrypted, Reveal), Watchdog Create, an AI opponent, the remaining Slot Effects (Revealed, Anchored, Banish...).
