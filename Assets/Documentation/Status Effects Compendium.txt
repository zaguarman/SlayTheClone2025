**Name:** Earworm
**Type:** Damage, Unique
**Description:** "Takes 1 damage Each Dusk. After 2 Days, spreads to adjacent friendlies if still present, then continues. Re-applying refreshes spread timer."
**Core Impact:** Persistent damage that can spread.
**Stacking & Duration:**
    **Initial Stacks:** 2 (acts as spread timer, actual damage is persistent until cleansed)
    **Re-application Behavior:** Refresh Stacks (refreshes the 2-Day spread timer)
**Turn-Based Effects:**
    **Trigger:** Each Dusk
    **Effect:** This creature takes 1 damage. If this status has 0 stacks remaining (i.e., after two instances of damage and check since last refresh/application), it spreads to adjacent friendly creatures (they gain `Earworm` with 2 stacks). The original `Earworm` then has its stacks reset to 2 (to continue the 2-Day spread cycle) and remains on the creature.
**Cleansable:** Yes (Cleansing prevents current instance from spreading if done before its stacks reach 0).
