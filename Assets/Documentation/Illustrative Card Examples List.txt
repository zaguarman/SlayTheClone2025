Card Name: Hostile Takeover Specialist
Type: Creature
Faction: Corporate
Stats: 1 Attack / 2 Health / 3 Speed / Deployment Time: 2
Ability (Active - Queue this action): Target an enemy creature with Health 2 or less (chosen when this action is queued). As an additional cost when this action resolves, this creature takes damage equal to the target's Health (determined at resolution). If this creature survives and an empty friendly slot is available, permanently gain control of the target creature. It enters an empty friendly slot. If no empty slot or this creature is destroyed by the cost, the action fails.
Flavor Text: "They weren't 'liquidated,' they were 'synergistically integrated' into our portfolio after a 'valuation adjustment'."
